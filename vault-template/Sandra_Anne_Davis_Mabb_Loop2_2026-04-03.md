This is now the strongest identified descendant pathway for Sandra's family. Confidence: Moderate, strengthening.

### 2. Noble Family: Jack Noble/Wannamutta ADB Biography Expanded

The ADB confirms Jack Noble was born Sandy Cape, K'gari (Fraser Island). Parents: Jacko Morris and Carie Martin (Butchulla). Recruited age 14 into Queensland Native Mounted Police. Deployed to Victoria in 1879 to track the Kelly Gang. Present at the Siege of Glenrowan, 29 June 1880. Married Alberta Clift (Quandamooka, Stradbroke Island) at Bogimbah mission. Customarily adopted Alberta's son George, who took the surname Noble.

This means Sandra's maternal great-grandfather was present at one of the most significant events in Australian colonial history.
