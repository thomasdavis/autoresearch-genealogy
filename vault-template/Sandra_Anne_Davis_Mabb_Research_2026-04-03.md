#### Fred and Phyllis Mabb Murder (2009)
Fred Mabb (age 82, b. ~1927) and Phyllis Mabb (age 71, b. ~1938) were murdered at 78 Pelican Road, Townview, Mount Isa on 15 Aug 2009. Their daughter Judith Prosser discovered them 17 Aug. Donald Tommy George (age 18) convicted, two life sentences. These are next-generation Mabbs, likely Fred's son (also named Fred) and his wife.

#### Peter Mabb (Mount Isa)
