# Noble Family of Mona Mona Mission: Sandra Anne Davis's Maternal Line

## Summary

Dorothy Molly Noble (b. 24 Jul 1918, d. 6 Sep 1950, Cairns) was Sandra Anne Davis's mother. She married Charles Joseph "Chooka" Davis on 2 October 1935 (QLD BDM 1935/C/3890). Dorothy was the daughter of George Clifford Noble (1888-1944) and Mary "Molly" Hewison (c.1893-1962), who married at Yarrabah Aboriginal Community on 8 April 1912 and were subsequently removed to Mona Mona Mission in 1914.

## Noble Family Tree (Sandra's Maternal Line)

### Generation 1: Jack Noble / Wannamutta (c.1855-1935)

- **Original name**: Wannamutta
- **Born**: Sandy Cape, K'gari (Fraser Island), Queensland
- **Parents**: Jacko Morris and Carie Martin (Butchulla/Budjula people)
- **Ethnicity**: Butchulla Aboriginal
- **Married**: Alberta Roberts/Clift (c.1878-1908), a Quandamooka (Jandai) woman from Stradbroke Island, married at Dunwich
- **Career**: Taken from family at age 14 by Constable Thomas King; put to work as trooper with Queensland Native Mounted Police. Later discharged.
- **Removal**: 3 October 1902, Jack and Alberta removed to Yarrabah Mission near Cairns
- **Death**: 15 January 1935, Pialba, Fraser Coast Region, QLD
- **Buried**: Polson Cemetery, Point Vernon, Fraser Coast Region, QLD (Plot F-27)
- **FindAGrave**: Memorial 105705613
- **Confidence**: Strong (ADB biography, FindAGrave, removal records)

Source: https://ia.anu.edu.au/biography/noble-jack-31647
Source: https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/105705613

**Note on relationship to Rev. James Noble**: Jack Noble (Wannamutta) of Fraser Island is a different person from Rev. James Noble (c.1876-1941, born near Boulia), who was the first Aboriginal Anglican clergyman. They share the surname Noble but appear to be from different language groups (Butchulla vs. unknown group near Boulia). Rev. James Noble married Angelina (c.1879-1964) and worked at Yarrabah, Forrest River, and other missions. His connection to the Mona Mona Noble family is not established.

### Generation 2: George Clifford Noble (1888-1944)

- **Born**: 1888, Queensland, Australia
- **Parents**: Jack Noble (d. 1935) and Alberta Roberts Noble (1878-1908)
- **Ethnicity**: Butchulla Aboriginal
- **Married**: Mary "Molly" Hewison (c.1893-1962), 8 April 1912, Yarrabah Aboriginal Community, QLD
- **Removal to Mona Mona**: 1914 (with wife Molly and child George). Reason recorded: "Interfering with dormitary [sic] girls"

- **Death**: 2 January 1944, Cairns Region, QLD
- **Buried**: Cairns Cemetery, Section MWS, Row L3, Site 4591
- **FindAGrave**: Memorial 168650570
- **Confidence**: Strong (FindAGrave, CIFHS exemption records, removal records, marriage records)

## Noble-Pitt Connection

### Confirmed Records

1. **William Pitt married Annie Noble, 14 May 1920, Yarrabah** (CIFHS registration 1920/C/1095; CPA Marriage Application 1920, Yarrabah). This is the earliest confirmed Noble-Pitt marriage.

2. **Agnes Norma Loretta Noble (b. 1916, Mona Mona) married a Pitt** (per Thompson genealogy and family sources; Noble-Pitt connection confirmed by DNA analysis).

### Analysis

Annie Noble who married William Pitt at Yarrabah in 1920 may be related to the Mona Mona Noble family. The George and Molly Noble family was transferred from Yarrabah to Mona Mona in 1914, but other Noble family members may have remained at Yarrabah. Annie could be:
- A sister or aunt of George Clifford Noble
- An unrelated Noble from the Yarrabah community

The Pitt family at Yarrabah (William Pitt) and the Pitt who married Agnes Norma Loretta Noble may or may not be the same family. The Pitt entries in CIFHS exemption records show Douglas, Brances, Milia, and Tom Pitt at Thursday Island (1937), and Jackie Pitt at Cunnamulla (1938). The FNQ Pitt family warrants further investigation.

**Confidence**: Moderate (marriage record confirmed; family link to Agnes Noble's Pitt is speculative without additional records)

## Key Findings from This Research

### Strong Signal

1. **George Clifford Noble and Molly Hewison were removed from Yarrabah to Mona Mona in 1914**, with a child named George. This is the earliest confirmed presence of the Noble family at Mona Mona. (CIFHS removal records)

2. **George and Molly Noble received exemption certificates at Mona Mona in 1922.** George's exemption was still listed as held in 1947. (CIFHS exemption registers)

3. **Dorothy Molly Noble married Charles Joseph Davis on 2 October 1935** (QLD BDM 1935/C/3890). (Previously established)

4. **George Gregory Noble (Dorothy's brother) married Alice McKenzie in 1935 at Cairns.** (CPA Marriage Applications)

5. **William Pitt married Annie Noble at Yarrabah in 1920** (CIFHS 1920/C/1095). This is the earliest documented Noble-Pitt connection.

6. **Charles Joseph Davis's exemption (Mossman, 1938) lists his parents as "H/C Phillipino, H/C Aborigine"**, confirming the Filipino-Aboriginal heritage on Sandra's paternal side. (CIFHS exemption register 1936-1941)

### Moderate Signal

7. **Jack Noble (Wannamutta, c.1855-1935) is confirmed as George Clifford Noble's father** via FindAGrave and ADB. He was Butchulla (Fraser Island) people, recruited into Native Mounted Police, removed to Yarrabah 1902. This gives Sandra's maternal line a Butchulla Aboriginal origin.

8. **The Noble family at Mona Mona is distinct from Rev. James Noble's family**, though both were at Yarrabah. Jack Noble was from Fraser Island; Rev. James Noble was from near Boulia/Normanton.

9. **Pansy Davis, Hazel Davis, Edgar Davis, and Kathleen were removed from Edmonton to Mona Mona in 1921.** This places a Davis family at Mona Mona before the Dorothy Noble/Charles Joseph Davis marriage in 1935, but these Davises appear to be from a different branch (Edgar Davis of Mossman/O'Shane line, not Charles Joseph Davis).

### Speculative

10. **Annie Noble (who married William Pitt in 1920 at Yarrabah) may be related to George Clifford Noble** and could be the link to the Noble-Pitt intermarriage pattern seen later with Agnes Norma Loretta Noble marrying a Pitt. Requires further investigation.

11. **Mary Ann Noble (married Paddy Leonard at Palm Island, 1937) could be another sibling of Dorothy**, as her marriage was registered under Aboriginal provisions (C/ registration). Unconfirmed.
