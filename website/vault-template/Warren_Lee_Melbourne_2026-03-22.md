The death notice mentions a wife named Lois and a son named Jason. The target Warren Lee's known child is Thomas Alwyn Davis (different surname, different first name). There is no mention of a Thomas or a Davis in the death notice.

Key factors suggesting this may be a **different** Warren Lee:

- The wife's name is Lois. No prior records link the target Warren Lee to a wife named Lois.
- Warren Lee is an extremely common name, particularly among people of Chinese heritage.
- The target Warren Lee was from Queensland; this death occurred in Melbourne, Victoria (though relocation is possible).

Key factors that cannot yet rule out a match:
- The target Warren Lee has been missing 10+ years; he could have remarried (Lois) and had another child (Jason) during that time.
- If born 1966, he would have been 55 at death in December 2021, a plausible age for a hospital death.
- Relocation from Queensland to Victoria is common.
- The death notice does not mention ethnicity or age, so we cannot confirm or exclude based on those factors.
