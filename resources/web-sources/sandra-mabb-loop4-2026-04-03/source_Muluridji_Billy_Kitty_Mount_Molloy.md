# Muluridji Native Title Claim: Billy and Kitty at Mount Molloy

## Finding

The Muluridji People #3 Native Title Claim lists apical ancestors including "Billy and Kitty (parents of Jimmy Riley)" in the Mount Molloy area.

## Connection to Sandra Anne Davis

Caroline Rose Roberts/Brown, known as "Kaitchi," was Sandra Anne Davis's paternal grandmother. She was from Mount Molloy, and her parents were named Billy and Kitty.

The Muluridji claim's "Billy and Kitty (parents of Jimmy Riley)" at Mount Molloy may be the same Billy and Kitty who are Caroline Rose's parents. If so, this would:

1. Identify Caroline Rose's Aboriginal language group as Muluridji (Djabuganjdji subgroup or related)
2. Connect Sandra's paternal grandmother to a specific native title claim
3. Identify Jimmy Riley as Caroline Rose's sibling or half-sibling

## Confidence

Speculative. The name match (Billy and Kitty, Mount Molloy) is suggestive but not conclusive. Billy and Kitty are common names, and Mount Molloy had multiple Aboriginal families. Verification would require the full native title claim documentation or contact with the Muluridji prescribed body corporate.
