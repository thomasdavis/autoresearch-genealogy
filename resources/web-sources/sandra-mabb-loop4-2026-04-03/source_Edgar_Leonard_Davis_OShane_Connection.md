# Edgar Leonard Davis and Caroline Brown: The O'Shane Connection

## Key Finding

The Australian Dictionary of Biography entry for Gladys Dorothy O'Shane (born 22 September 1919, Mossman) states she was "the sixth child of Edgar Davis, labourer, and Caroline, nee Brown, both Queensland born."

This confirms:
1. Edgar Leonard Davis (b. 1911, Mossman, CIFHS exemption no. 27, 9 April 1938) married Caroline Brown
2. Caroline Brown is the same person recorded elsewhere as Caroline Rose Roberts/Brown, known as "Kaitchi," from Mount Molloy
3. Edgar Leonard Davis and Charles Joseph "Chooka" Davis (b. 1912) are brothers: both at Mossman, one year apart, same parents described as "H/C Phillipino, H/C Aborigine" (Charles) and "H/C, H/C" (Edgar)

## Implications for Sandra Anne Davis

If Edgar Leonard Davis married Caroline Brown (Kaitchi), and Charles Joseph Davis also had Caroline Rose as his mother (per the family tree), then either:

**Hypothesis A**: Edgar and Charles are both sons of George Edgar (Braikenridge) Davis and Caroline Rose Brown. Edgar married a different Caroline, or the ADB entry is describing a different generation.

**Hypothesis B**: The "Caroline nee Brown" in the ADB entry is Sandra's grandmother Caroline Rose, and Edgar is her husband, not her son. This would make Edgar Leonard Davis = George Edgar Davis under a different name. But the birth years conflict (Edgar b. 1911 vs George Edgar b. 1885-1890).

**Hypothesis C (most likely)**: Edgar Leonard Davis is a brother of Chooka, and he married a woman also named Caroline Brown (or the same woman after George Edgar's first partnership ended). The ADB says Gladys was born in 1919 to Edgar and Caroline. Charles (Chooka) was born 1912. Both are at Mossman.

This needs resolution via QLD BDM birth records for Edgar Leonard Davis and Charles Joseph Davis.

## Pat O'Shane Connection

Gladys Dorothy O'Shane's daughter **Patricia June "Pat" O'Shane** (born 19 June 1941, Mossman) became:
- Australia's first Aboriginal magistrate (appointed 1986)
- First Aboriginal person to head a NSW government department (1981)
- Chancellor of the University of New England (2005-2011)

If Edgar Leonard Davis is Charles Joseph Davis's brother, then Pat O'Shane is Sandra Anne Davis's first cousin once removed.
