## Home Secretary Correspondence: E Davis / Edgar Davis at Mona Mona

Four entries (1933-1934):
1. 1933: E Davis requests removal of his daughter from Rocky Point to Palm Island
2. 1933: Edgar Davis, Mona Mona, instruction at blind school
3. 1934: E Davis, blind boy
4. 1934: E Davis, blind, pass to return to Mona Mona

These connect an "Edgar Davis" to Mona Mona Mission in the 1930s. If this is Edgar Leonard Davis (brother of Chooka), it shows he had connections to the same mission where Sandra's maternal family (the Nobles) lived. The "blind" entries suggest he had a blind child who received instruction.
