# Rosie Reynolds: Cemetery Burial Date Discrepancy

## CDFHS McLeod Street Pioneer Cemetery Record

- Name: Rosie Reynolds
- Burial number: 2113
- Burial date: 28 October 1914
- Born: Queensland
- Occupation: Servant
- Religion: Protestant
- Section: Mixed Denominations
- Grave: unmarked

Source: cdfhs.org/indexes/cemetery-registers/mcleod-street-cemetery-graves-index/

## Existing Research

Previous research states Rosie Reynolds (aka McIvor/Robinson) died 30 March 1904, buried Pioneer Cemetery, Cairns.

## Discrepancy

The cemetery index shows burial 28 October 1914, a full 10 years after the expected death date of 1904. Possible explanations:

1. Two different Rosie Reynolds buried at the same cemetery
2. The 1904 date is incorrect and she actually died in 1914
3. Data entry error in the cemetery index
4. Reburial or memorial entry

## CIFHS Aboriginal Deaths Register

A "Rosie, Cooktown, 14.2.1910" entry appears in the CIFHS Aboriginal Deaths register 1910-1936. If the 1904 death date is correct, this Rosie from Cooktown in 1910 would be a different person. But if Rosie Reynolds lived beyond 1904, the 1910 Cooktown entry could be yet another person, or a mistaken date.

## Resolution

Order the $8 full grave record from CDFHS (records@cdfhs.org) specifying burial number 2113. This should clarify the name, dates, and any additional details that distinguish this Rosie Reynolds from others.

## Significance

If Rosie Reynolds (Sandra's paternal great-grandmother) died in 1914 rather than 1904, she would have been alive for the first years of George Edgar Davis's life and possibly for the birth of other Braikenridge/Davis children. This would change the narrative of the family significantly.
