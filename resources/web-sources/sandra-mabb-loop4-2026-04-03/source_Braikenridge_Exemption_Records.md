# Braikenridge / Brackenridge Exemption Records

## Arthur Braikenridge

### 1908-1936 Register
- Name: Arthur Braikenridge
- Age: 20
- Location: Mossman
- Year: 1915

This is the earliest Braikenridge exemption, at the same Mossman location where Charles Joseph Davis and Edgar Leonard Davis later held exemptions. Arthur is likely a sibling or close relative of George Edgar (Braikenridge) Davis.

### 1942-1967 Register
- Name: Arthur Braikenridge
- Age: 47
- Status: married
- Location: Ingham
- Year: 1943
- Spouse: Florence (37)
- Children: Allan Henry L, Kathleen Haidee, Alma Atherlie, Arthur Kenneth, Doreen Florence, George Joseph, Lydia Rose, Ruth Marian

### CPA Correspondence
- Arthur Brackenridge, Palm Island, 1940, file 1003, "Palm Is Nats"

### 1947 Register
Arthur Braikenridge and Arthur Kenneth Braikenridge, both Ingham, 1943. Also Ruth Martin Braikenridge, Ingham, 1943.

## Fred Brackenridge

### 1942-1967 Register
- Name: Fred Brackenridge
- Age: 49
- Location: Ingham
- Year: 1946
- Spouse: Ivy (36)
- Children: Sylvia Ethel, Frederick Charles, Josephine

### 1947 Register
Charles Brackenridge (Ingham, 1946), Ethel Brackenridge, Fred Brackenridge, Frederick Brackenridge, Josephine Brackenridge, Sylvia Brackenridge (all Ingham, 1946).

## Edgar Braikenridge

### 1908-1936 Register
Listed as "Edgar Braikenridge, Mossman, 1915" in the 1947 active list.

## Significance

The Braikenridge family shows a clear pattern:
1. Early presence at Mossman (Arthur 1915, Edgar 1915)
2. Later concentration at Ingham (Arthur's family 1943, Fred's family 1946)
3. George Edgar appears to have dropped the Braikenridge surname in favour of Davis
4. Arthur's child "George Joseph" Braikenridge may be named after George Edgar
