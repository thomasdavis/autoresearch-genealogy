# Fred and Phyllis Mabb Murder: Court Record Extract

## Victims

- Frederick Mabb, age 82 (born c.1927)
- Phillis Mabb, age 71 (born c.1938)
- Address: 78 Pelican Road, Townview, Mount Isa

## Crime

On the night of 15 August 2009, Donald Tommy George (then 18, approximately 150 cm tall, with prior burglary/violence convictions) entered the Mabb home. Frederick sustained at least five blunt force head injuries and died of asphyxia. Phillis sustained six major blunt force head injuries, broken ribs, and a torn coronary artery; she survived eight days in hospital before dying of heart failure. George's DNA was found at over 30 locations in the home.

## Discovery

Daughter Judith Prosser found them on the morning of Monday 17 August 2009, just before 9 AM. Frederick was in the hallway between bedroom and lounge room; Phillis was on the lounge room floor.

## Sentencing

Donald Tommy George convicted 24 April 2012 at Mount Isa Supreme Court. Justice David North imposed two life sentences, calling it a "shocking and brutal crime against elderly, infirm, defenceless citizens." George became eligible for parole 28 August 2029 (after 971 days served). Appeal dismissed 20 September 2013 (R v George [2013] QCA 267).

## Family Connection

The only family member named is daughter Judith Prosser. No spouse names, parents, siblings, or extended family mentioned in court judgment or news.

Fred's birth year c.1927 fits as a child of Frederick Alfred Richard Mabb (b. c.1906, Cecil's brother, married Muriel Ivy Walker 1926). If confirmed, Fred would be Kenneth William Mabb's first cousin.

Cecil Maurice Mabb (d. 1985) is the only other Mabb in Mount Isa Sunset Lawn Cemetery. Fred and Phyllis not in that index.
