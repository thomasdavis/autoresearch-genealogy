# CIFHS Deep Search Results

## 463 Queensland pages downloaded to resources/cifhs/qld/

## KEY DISCOVERIES

### 1. Edgar Davis's Children REMOVED to Mona Mona

From Queensland Removals 1912-1939:

**"Pansy Davis (11), Hazel Davis (10), Edgar Davis (5), Kathleen (2 1/2) - Edmonton to Mona Mona - For their own good"**

Edgar's young children were removed from Edmonton to Mona Mona mission. "Edgar Davis (5)" = Edgar Leonard Davis (born c.1910). If removed when Edgar Leonard was 5, this happened c.1915. The other children:
- **Pansy Davis** (11) - born c.1904
- **Hazel Davis** (10) - born c.1905
- **Kathleen** (2.5) - born c.1912-1913

These are children of George Edgar Davis and Caroline Rose Molloy/Roberts.

### 2. George Noble Removal Confirmed

**"George Noble and wife Molly and child George - Yarrabah to Mona Mona - Interfering with dormitory girls"**

George Noble, his wife Molly, and their son George were removed from Yarrabah to Mona Mona. The reason "interfering with dormitory girls" is disturbing.

### 3. Willie Molloy Removal

Willie Molloy removed from Mossman - "addicted to opium, children to accompany their parents"

A Molloy at Mossman, removed for opium. Could be related to Caroline Rose Molloy.

### 4. Paul Murgha Removal to Fantome Island

**"Paul Murgha & wife Elizabeth A Murgha and their three children - Yarrabah to Fantome"**

A Murgha family removed to Fantome Island (leprosy colony). Paul Murgha is likely related to Jhinna/Jena Murgha (Nellie's husband).

### 5. David Molloy Death (June 1920, Mossman)

From QLD Deaths 1910-1928:

**"June 1920 - David Molloy, H, Mossman"**

A half-caste Molloy man died at Mossman in 1920. If Caroline Rose Molloy/Roberts was from the Mossman Molloy family, David could be her brother, father, or relative.

### 6. Deaths Register - Key Rosie Entries

- **14 Feb 1910: Rosie, F, Cooktown** - Full Blood, Cooktown
- **17 Mar 1916: Rosie, F, Port Douglas Hospital** - Full Blood, Port Douglas

### 7. Julia's Wedding Witnesses (Yarrabah Marriages)

Julia Nunn married Victor Leftwich 15 Feb 1912. Witnesses:
- **George Noble**
- **Nellie**

Mother listed as **"Rosie Rosie"**

George Noble had married Molly Abreson 9 months earlier (11 May 1911). George was about 18, Nellie was about 15 (if born 1897).

### 8. Nellie's Marriage Application (Card Index 1934-1941)

**Dan Owens, Born 1894, H/C married Nellie Murgha, Born 1897, H/C in 1941**

Nellie's birth year listed as **1897** on her own marriage application. This contradicts the 1905 removal listing her as age 4.5 (which would put birth at 1900-1901).

If born 1897:
- She was 8 at the 1905 removal (listed as 4.5 - error)
- She was 9 at the 1906 baptism (listed as ~9 - correct)
- She was 15 at Julia's 1912 wedding (plausible witness)
- She was 44 at her 1941 marriage

### 9. Bessie Leftwich in Cairns (1938)

**"Bessie Leftwick Cairns 1938 2290 Emp Cty Nats"** (Chief Protector card)

Bessie Rosina Leftwich (Julia's daughter, named after Rosie) was in Cairns in 1938 under employment/country natives records.

## YARRABAH AS THE HUB

Everyone converged at Yarrabah:
- Edgar Davis (baptised 1896, married 1909, writing articles 1908)
- Julia Nunn (baptised 1906, married Victor Leftwich 1912)
- Nellie Watkin (baptised 1906, married Jhinna Murgha, witnessed Julia's wedding 1912)
- George Noble (married Molly 1911, witnessed Julia's wedding 1912, removed to Mona Mona)
- Jhinna/Jena Murgha (WW1 census, married Nellie, children from 1917)
- Victor Leftwich (married Julia 1912, children from 1914)

Then many were removed to Mona Mona:
- George Noble + Molly + child George
- Willie Molloy
- Edgar's children (Pansy, Hazel, Edgar Leonard, Kathleen)

And Nellie stayed at Yarrabah with the Murgha family until marrying Dan Owens in 1941.
