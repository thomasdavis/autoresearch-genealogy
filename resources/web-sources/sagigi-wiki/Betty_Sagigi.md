# Betty Sagigi

## Identity

Torres Strait Islander Indigenous Health Worker based on Thursday Island. Known as "Auntie Betty Sagigi" in community contexts. Key figure in Torres Strait aged care and dementia research.

## Career

### Aged Care Assessment Team Coordinator and Assessor

Betty Sagigi is the **Aged Care Assessment Team (ACAT) Coordinator and Assessor** for the Torres Strait and Northern Peninsula Area, working as part of Thursday Island's Primary Health Post-Acute Rehab and Aged Care Program within Torres and Cape Hospital and Health Services. She has held this role for approximately 10 years.

### Prior Roles

- Managed **Commonwealth Funding Agreements** for the Primary Health Care Indigenous Health Programs.
- Managed initiatives under the **Queensland State Government Department of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Policy**.

## Student of the Year 2014

Betty Sagigi was named the **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Student of the Year** in 2014, as reported in the **Torres News** (Thursday Island, Qld.: 1957-2015) on 31 March 2014. The article is archived on Trove (National Library of Australia).

## Research with JCU: Healthy Ageing Research Team (HART)

Betty Sagigi is an integral part of the **Healthy Ageing Research Team (HART)** at James Cook University (JCU). Her contributions include:

### Publications (co-author)

1. **"Following in Elders' Footsteps: Yarning About Ageing Well in the Torres Strait"** (2025)
   - Authors: Rachel Quigley, Chenoa Wapau, Betty Sagigi, Sarah G. Russell, Sean Taylor, Sarah Larkins, Edward Strivens, Michelle Redman-MacLaren
   - Published in: Qualitative Health Research (SAGE Journals), 2025
   - DOI: 10.1177/10497323251335210

2. **"The prevalence of falls and associated factors in older adults of the Torres Strait"**
   - Published in PMC (PubMed Central)

3. **"Culturally Appropriate Assessment of Depression and Anxiety in Older Torres Strait Islanders: Limitations and Recommendations"**
   - Published in: Clinical Gerontologist, Vol 46, No 2

4. **"How First Nations peoples living in the Torres Strait and Northern Peninsula Area describe and discuss social and emotional well-being"** (2024)
   - Published in: Australian Journal of Rural Health

5. **Dementia Prevalence study:**
   - Published in: Australasian Journal on Ageing (2021)
   - Betty was involved from design through data collection to knowledge translation.

### Role in Research

Betty has ensured that screening tools for dementia are **culturally appropriate**, facilitated **maximum community participation**, and ensured that community consultation and engagement is conducted in a **culturally safe and appropriate way**.

### Community Education

Auntie Betty Sagigi and Chenoa Wapau yarn about how memory and thinking can change with age and talk about dementia, which is not a normal part of ageing. This public health messaging is part of the HART team's knowledge translation work.
