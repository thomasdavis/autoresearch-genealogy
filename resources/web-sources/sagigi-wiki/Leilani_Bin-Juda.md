# Leilani Bin-Juda

## Identity

Proud Torres Strait Islander woman with ties to Hammond, Darnley and Murray Islands. Described as a descendant of Mareja Doolah/Bora Bin Juda (per research request; the Doolah-Bin Juda family connection was not independently confirmed in web searches but is consistent with her Darnley Island heritage matching the Doolah family's Erub/Darnley roots).

## Education

- Grew up in Cairns.
- **Bachelor of Business, CQUniversity (1995):** Began academic journey at CQUniversity in Rockhampton.
- Also holds degrees in **Business, Cultural Heritage and International Relations**.

## Career Timeline

### Early Public Service

- Began career in the Australian Public Service with the then Department of Primary Industries and Energy in Canberra.
- Followed by the Attorney-General's Department.

### Peter Mitchell Churchill Fellowship (2000)

Awarded the **Peter Mitchell Churchill Fellowship** to investigate strategies museums had implemented to engage Indigenous people in exhibition development, with a particular focus on youth. Research conducted in New Zealand, Canada, the United States and Hawaii.

### Museum and Cultural Heritage Work

- **Curator, National Museum of Australia.**
- **Established the Gab Titui Cultural Centre** on Thursday Island, a significant Torres Strait Islander cultural institution.

### Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT)

Career officer with DFAT for over 10 years (from approximately 2007). Overseas postings include:

1. **Manager, Australia's Cultural Program at the Shanghai World Expo 2010.**
2. **Adviser (Economic Governance), Regional Assistance Mission to Solomon Islands (RAMSI), Honiara.**
3. **First Secretary (Political/Gender/Sports Diplomacy), Australian High Commission, Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea.**
4. **Torres Strait Treaty Liaison Officer, Thursday Island:** First Torres Strait Islander in this role. Responsible for day-to-day implementation of the Australia-Papua New Guinea Torres Strait Treaty. Worked with fellow Torres Strait Islander Ugarie Mene; first time the office had been staffed by an all-islander team.

### CEO, Torres Strait Regional Authority (TSRA)

- Appointed as TSRA CEO, the **first woman to be substantively appointed** to the role.
- Served as CEO until January 2022.

### High Commissioner to Nigeria (November 2023)

- Appointed Australia's **High Commissioner to the Federal Republic of Nigeria** in November 2023.
- **First Indigenous Australian** to hold a High Commissioner role.
- Also accredited to the Republics of Benin, Cameroon, Niger, The Gambia and Gabon.

## Awards and Honours

- **Public Service Medal (PSM), 2019:** For promoting the inclusion of Indigenous heritage in Australia's cultural and foreign policies.
- **Peter Mitchell Churchill Fellowship, 2000.**

## Community Roles

- Financial mentoring of other Indigenous women (featured in "The Joyful Frugalista" podcast, Episode 73).
- Member of the Indigenous Diplomacy program (CDU).
- ANZSOG (Australia and New Zealand School of Government) connection.
