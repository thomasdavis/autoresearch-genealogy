# Reuben Doolah

## Identity

Torres Strait Islander dancer based in Sydney. Heritage from Murray Island (Mer) and Darnley Island (Erub) in the Torres Strait, North Queensland. One of Sydney's most experienced traditional Aboriginal and Torres Strait Island dancers.

Note: The birth year of 1927 was provided by the research request but could not be independently confirmed through web searches. Given his professional career starting in 1986, this date seems plausible but should be verified.

## Dance Education

- Studied dance at **NAISDA College** (National Aboriginal and Islander Skills Development Association) in Sydney.
- Finished his degree at the **Aboriginal Dance Theatre** in Redfern, Sydney.

NAISDA Dance College is Australia's premier Indigenous training college for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander performers. It was founded in 1976 at Glebe by Carole Y. Johnson, an African-American modern dancer.

## Professional Career

Has been performing professionally since **1986**.

### Ngaru Aboriginal and Torres Strait Dance Company (1993)

Foundation member of the **Ngaru Aboriginal and Torres Strait Dance Company**, Sydney's first independent professional Indigenous dance troupe, established in 1993. "Ngaru" means "shake a leg," a dance style prevalent in the Cape York Peninsula in Far North Queensland.

### Descendance Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Dance Theatre

Descendance was born from the Ngaru Dance Company. Reuben is a member of Descendance, which continues the tradition of professional Torres Strait Islander and Aboriginal dance performance.

## Notable Performances

- **Honolulu Festival:** Performed before an audience of 500,000 people on a floating moving stage down Waikiki.
- **"Red Centre Dreaming" Tour (2002):** Covered Italy, England, Scotland, Ireland, Germany, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, and France. Performed to over 100,000 people in over 300 live appearances.
- **Queen's Jubilee (2002):** Performed for Queen Elizabeth II during the Jubilee celebrations as part of the Red Centre Dreaming tour.

## Teaching

Reuben is also a dance teacher and conducts workshops in schools around Australia in both traditional and contemporary dance styles.
