# Elia Doolah (Snr)

## Identity

Born 5 January 1924 on Erub (Darnley Island), Torres Strait. Died 2 August 1984 in Brisbane. Torres Strait Islander serviceman, church minister, and community leader.

## WWII Service: Torres Strait Light Infantry Battalion

Private Elia (Snr) Doolah served in the Torres Strait Light Infantry Battalion during World War II.

The Torres Strait Light Infantry Battalion was the only Indigenous Australian battalion ever formed by the Australian Army. Initially raised as a company-sized unit in 1941, it was expanded to a full battalion in 1942. Almost all enlisted men were Torres Strait Islanders.

Recruits from Mer, Erub, Masig and Ugar formed A Company of the battalion. The first volunteers came from Erub and Mer Islands, enlisting in June 1941 after Prime Minister Curtin gave authority for raising a Company of Torres Strait Islander men in May 1941.

The AWM holds a record for Private Elia (Snr) Doolah at collection reference P11077601.

## Post-War Life: Universal World Church

Elia Doolah (Snr) was a minister of the Universal World Church. A 1973 photograph from Townsville shows Universal World Church Ministers including "Uncle Rete Doolah" among the members, indicating broader Doolah family involvement with this church. The church was significant among Torres Strait Islander communities on the mainland.

He was referred to as "Big Daddy Elia" in family and community references.

## Community and Political Roles

Note: Some ATSIC/TSRA references to "Elia Doolah" appear to refer to a different, later individual (possibly Elia Doolah Jnr or another family member), given the dates involved:

- An "Elia Doolah" was elected ATSIC Commissioner for the Torres Strait in April 2000, was Chairperson of Erub (Darnley) Island Council, TSRA Portfolio Member for Education, Training and Employment, Chairperson of the Area Consultative Committee, Executive member on the ICC, and a member of the IBIS Board.

Since Elia Doolah (Snr) died in 1984, these 2000-era roles must belong to a different Elia Doolah, likely a son or namesake (Elia Doolah Jnr). This confirms the existence of an Elia Doolah Jnr who was active in Torres Strait community leadership into the 2000s.
