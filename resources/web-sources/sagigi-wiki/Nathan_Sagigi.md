# Nathan Sagigi

## Identity

Daurareb and Wakaid man from Murray Island and Badu Island in the Torres Strait. Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (Honours) student at the University of Queensland.

## Heritage

- **Daurareb:** Connection to Murray Island (Mer) in the Eastern Torres Strait.
- **Wakaid:** Connection to Badu Island in the Western Torres Strait.

## Education Journey

- Grew up on Thursday Island in the Torres Strait.
- Relocated from his home in the Torres Strait to Brisbane to study at UQ.
- Enrolled in a Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (Honours) at the University of Queensland.

## Scholarship

Nathan received a **Geoffrey Huey Sattler Indigenous Scholarship**, established in 2019 to create opportunities for First Nations peoples to study at UQ in perpetuity. The scholarship enabled him to relocate from the Torres Strait and begin building his career.

## Motivation

Nathan observed chronic disease in his community on Thursday Island and found the passion to help people with chronic disease and prevent others from developing it. His goal is to return to Thursday Island where he can help improve the lives of his family and friends through exercise physiology.
