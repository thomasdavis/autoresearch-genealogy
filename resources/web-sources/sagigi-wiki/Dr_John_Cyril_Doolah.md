# Dr John "Cyril" Doolah

## Identity

Proud Torres Strait Islander of **Erubam le** (Erub/Darnley Island person) and **Meriam le** (Mer/Murray Island person) heritage. Belongs to the **sager people of Mer**. His Mer **nosik** (clan division) is **Samsep-Meriam**.

## Academic Career

### Education

- **Diploma** (field not specified)
- **Double Degrees** (fields not specified)
- **Master of Arts**
- **PhD (English), University of Newcastle, 2021**

### PhD Thesis

Title: **"The stories behind the Torres Strait Islander migration myth: the journey of the sap/bethey"**

The thesis focuses on decolonising the migration and urbanisation of Torres Strait Islanders (Ailan pipel) from the Torres Straits to mainland Australia between the 1960s and 1970s. The thesis is held at the University of Newcastle repository (nova.newcastle.edu.au).

### Academic Positions

1. **Wollotuka School of Aboriginal Studies, University of Newcastle:** Lectured in Aboriginal studies. Listed as staff at the University of Newcastle.

2. **Ngarrngga Postdoctoral Fellow, University of Melbourne:** Held a postdoctoral fellowship in the Faculty of Education.

3. **Lecturer in Indigenous Education, Melbourne Graduate School of Education, University of Melbourne (current):** Lectures on Torres Strait Island knowledge and Indigenous education.
