# Marita Sagigi

## Identity

Proud Dauareb descendant from the Eastern Islands of the Torres Strait. Senior scientist and health executive on Thursday Island.

## Career Timeline

1. **Year 10 work experience student** at the Hospital and Health Service: Marita's career began as a Year 10 work experience student at the HHS. She is passionate about Torres Strait Islanders having access to a career in health.

2. **Medical Scientist, Thursday Island Pathology Laboratory (from 2005):** Worked on Thursday Island since 2005 as a Medical Scientist at the Thursday Island Pathology laboratory. Held the title of Senior Scientist in the Pathology unit.

3. **Operations Manager, Primary Health Care (June 2020):** Joined Torres and Cape Hospital and Health Service in June 2020 as Operations Manager Primary Health Care.

4. **Manager of Corporate Services North (January 2021):** Became Manager of Corporate Services North.

5. **Executive General Manager, Northern Sector, TCHHS (current):** Serves as Executive General Manager for the Northern Sector of the Torres and Cape Hospital and Health Service (TCHHS).

## Awards and Recognition

- **Torres Shire Council Australia Day Award:** Recognised for her hard work in improving pathology services in Queensland's northern sector, particularly her work in the Pathology Laboratory on Thursday Island.
- **Queensland Health recognition:** A shoutout from Queensland Health on social media for her work and her colleagues' work in the pathology laboratory on Thursday Island.
- **Quality Improvement Excellence Award nomination (2016):** Her team nominated the Badu Island Health Check team for the TCHHS Quality Improvement Excellence Award.
