# Mistee Sagigi

## Identity

Rising AFLW talent from Thursday Island, Torres Strait. Member of the Gold Coast Suns Academy (StreetSmarts SUNS Academy). Tagai State College student.

## AFL Career Timeline

1. **First picked up a Sherrin** through the AFL Cape York development program at Tagai State College on Thursday Island. Despite only playing her first competitive match approximately two years before her 2024 breakthrough, her natural talent and determination set her apart.

2. **Gold Coast Suns Academy:** Scouted by the Gold Coast SUNS franchise and became part of the StreetSmarts SUNS Academy program.

3. **AFL Woomeras 2024 (July 2024):** Selected among 38 Indigenous players to participate in the AFL's 2024 Woomeras program, hosted in Brisbane at Brighton Homes Arena on 13 July. The Woomeras program is recognised as one of the key steps for players in the national talent pathway. Sagigi performed strongly throughout the game, with her second efforts, hard-tackling style and confidence a standout. She sealed the Woomeras' win with the final goal of the game and was awarded the team's best-on-ground honours.

4. **2024 Troy Clarke Scholarship:** Awarded the Troy Clarke Scholarship, named in honour of the late Troy Clarke, a former Brisbane Bears player and AFL Queensland Development Manager. The scholarship recognises young athletes from regional Queensland who demonstrate qualities of dedication, passion, and respect.

5. **Gold Coast Suns Under-17 Women's Team:** Played for the Gold Coast Suns U17 women's team in national development squad matches.

6. **Injury Setback (2025):** Suffered a mild concussion during a match against the Sydney Swans, forcing her out of at least one of two remaining national development squad matches. She was 16 years old at the time. The forced break gave her perspective: "I'm very excited, and I want it more, because this is my first time having a concussion and actually stepping away from AFL. I think it's made me a better player."

7. **AFL Cape York recognition:** Continued involvement in AFL Cape York programs and schools competitions.
