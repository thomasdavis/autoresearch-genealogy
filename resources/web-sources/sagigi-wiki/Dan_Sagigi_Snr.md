# Dan Sagigi (Snr)

## Identity

Traditional Badulaig elder from Badu Island, Torres Strait. Original named applicant in the Badu Island native title claim on behalf of the Badulgal people.

## Native Title Claim

Dan Sagigi (Snr) was the original applicant in the native title claim made on behalf of the people of Badu Island in the Torres Strait. Following his death in July 2002, Mr Victor Nona proceeded as the named applicant on behalf of the Badulgal.

The Badulgal people's ownership of Badu and surrounding islands in the Torres Strait was recognised in a native title determination on 1 February 2014, when the Queensland Government handed over to the Badulgal traditional owners freehold title to 9,836 hectares (24,310 acres) of land.

The Prescribed Body Corporate that resulted from this determination is the Mura Badulgal (Torres Strait Islanders) Corporation RNTBC, which manages Badulgal native title rights.

## Death

Dan Sagigi (Snr) died in July 2002, before the native title determination was finalised. His passing was noted in legal proceedings as the reason for the change of named applicant to Victor Nona.

## Community Context

Badu Island (also known as Mulgrave Island) is a Torres Strait island with a population of approximately 800 people. The Badulgal people are the traditional owners. The island community maintains strong cultural ties and governance through the Torres Strait Island Regional Council and the Mura Badulgal RNTBC.

## Family Connections

Robert "Bongo" Sagigi is known from Torres Strait Islanders Media Association interviews. The broader Sagigi family has deep roots on Badu Island.
