# Salapata Sagigi

## Identity

Torres Strait Islander actor from Thursday Island. Known for his role in the 1954 Australian film "King of the Coral Sea."

## Film: King of the Coral Sea (1954)

### Production Details

- **Director:** Lee Robinson
- **Writers:** Lee Robinson and Chips Rafferty
- **Shot on location:** Thursday Island, Torres Strait
- **World premiere:** 17 July 1954, held simultaneously on Thursday Island and in Melbourne
- **Genre:** Adventure, Crime
- **IMDb rating:** 6.6

### Cast

- **Chips Rafferty** as Ted King (pearler)
- **Charles "Bud" Tingwell** as Peter Merriman
- **Ilma Adey** as Rusty (Ted's daughter)
- **Rod Taylor** as Jack Janiero (Ted's offsider; this was Rod Taylor's film debut)
- **Salapata Sagigi** as **Charlie Juda**
- **Jack Assam**
- **Charlie Juda** (also appears as a cast member name, suggesting Salapata Sagigi may have played a character named after a real local person)
- **Frances Chin Soon**

### Plot

Ted King (Chips Rafferty), a pearler on Thursday Island in the Torres Strait, finds a body floating in the water. Ted and his offsider, Jack Janiero (Rod Taylor), discover that head diver Yusep and town drunk Grundy are involved in a people smuggling operation. When Ted's daughter Rusty (Ilma Adey) is kidnapped by the smugglers, Ted and Merriman, a big city playboy and owner of Ted's company (Charles Tingwell), join forces to rescue her.

### Significance

The film is notable for being shot entirely on location on Thursday Island, providing a rare visual record of life in the Torres Strait in the 1950s. It featured local Torres Strait Islander people in the cast, including Salapata Sagigi. The film is held in the collection of the **National Film and Sound Archive of Australia (NFSA)**.

The character name "Charlie Juda" played by Sagigi is noteworthy as "Juda" is a Torres Strait Islander family name (cf. Bora Bin Juda, the Bin-Juda family). This may indicate the character was based on or named after a real local person.
