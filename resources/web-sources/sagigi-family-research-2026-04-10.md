# Sagigi Family Research: Torres Strait Islands

Date: 2026-04-10

## Summary

The Sagigi surname is a Torres Strait Islander family name with strong connections to Badu Island (western Torres Strait) and Murray Island / Mer (eastern Torres Strait). The family has deep roots in the region, with Dan Sagigi (Snr) serving as the original native title applicant for Badu Island. Multiple family members are publicly documented in health, sport, film, music, and community leadership roles.

## Traditional Island Connections

### Badu Island (Primary)
- Dan Sagigi (Snr), traditional Badulaig elder, was the original applicant in the native title claim for Badu Island. He died in July 2002; Victor Nona then continued as named applicant. Native title was determined on 1 February 2014.
- Robert "Bongo" Sagigi is a Wakaid Elder on Badu Island, involved in community health initiatives and media (interviewed by Torres Strait Islanders Media Association about Survival Day).
- Royston Sagigi-Baira's father is from Badu Island.
- Nathan Sagigi identifies as Wakaid (the only town on Badu Island is Wakaid, on the south-east coast).

### Murray Island / Mer (Secondary)
- Nathan Sagigi identifies as Daurareb and Wakaid, from both Murray Island and Badu Island. Daurareb appears to correspond to the Dauer/Piadram tribe, one of the eight tribes on Mer.
- Jessie Sagigi told the story "Paslag" to Margaret Lawrie, collected from the Mer/Erub/Ugar island group (eastern Torres Strait). This is documented in the Margaret Lawrie Torres Strait Islands Collection at the State Library of Queensland (UNESCO Memory of the World).

### Thursday Island (Administrative Hub)
- Multiple Sagigi family members have lived and worked on Thursday Island, which serves as the administrative centre for the Torres Strait.

### Wagadagam Connection
- Royston Sagigi-Baira identifies as Wagadagam (Torres Strait Islander) and Thanakwith (Aboriginal). Wagadagam is a clan associated with Mabuiag Island (near western group), with the crocodile as its totem.

## Variant Spelling Search Results

- **Sagigi**: Primary and consistent spelling across all modern records
- **Sagagai**: No results as a Torres Strait surname
- **Sagai**: Not found as a surname. "Sagai" is a Torres Strait mythological/ancestral figure: one of four brothers (Malo, Sagai, Kulka, Siu) who paddled to the central and eastern islands from Cape York. Sagai landed at Iama Island (Yam), assumed god-like status, crocodile totem. This cultural connection to crocodile totem may be relevant to the Wagadagam clan association.
- **Sagigi-Baira**: Hyphenated double-barrel form used by Royston (combining Sagigi with Baira)

## Cultural and Clan Context

The Sagigi family appears to bridge two Torres Strait cultural groups:
1. **Badulgal** (western Torres Strait, Badu Island): Dan Sagigi Snr was a traditional Badulaig elder. The Badulgal are part of the broader Kala Lagaw Ya language group.
2. **Meriam** (eastern Torres Strait, Murray Island/Mer): Nathan Sagigi identifies Daurareb clan ties to Mer. The Meriam are a Melanesian people with their own language (Meriam Mir).
3. **Wagadagam** (near western, Mabuiag Island area): Royston Sagigi-Baira identifies as Wagadagam. Crocodile totem.

This multi-island affiliation is common in Torres Strait families due to inter-island marriage and movement patterns.
