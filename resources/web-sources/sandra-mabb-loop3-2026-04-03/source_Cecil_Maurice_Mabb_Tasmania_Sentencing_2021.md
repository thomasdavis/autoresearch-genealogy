# Cecil Maurice Mabb: Supreme Court of Tasmania Sentencing Record

## Identity

- Full name: Cecil Maurice Mabb
- Age at sentencing: 42 years old (sentencing in late 2021, placing birth at approximately 1979)
- Named after his paternal grandfather Cecil Maurice Mabb (1908-1985, Mount Isa)

## Family Background

- Mother: described as "a member of the stolen generation" who "succumbed to heavy abuse of alcohol and was unable to care for him"
- Father: relocated with Cecil to Tasmania at age 5 (approximately 1984)
- Has two children; estranged after 2019 marital breakdown
- Was married; relationship broke down in 2019

## Residential History

1. Born approximately 1979 (location not stated, likely Far North Queensland)
2. Moved to Tasmania with father at age 5 (approximately 1984)
3. At age 13 (approximately 1992), claims sexual abuse and abandonment
4. Moved to Queensland, "exposed to abuse of alcohol and drugs"
5. Between 1995 and 2003 (ages 16-24), committed sexual offences against children in Queensland
6. 2008: sentenced to 4 years imprisonment in Queensland; served 16 months
7. Mid-2020: moved to Tasmania again
8. Lived in Rosebery (Tasmania) before arrest
9. Caravan and shed destroyed by vigilantes after arrest

## Criminal History

- 1995-2003: series of sexual offences against children (Queensland)
- Pre-2008: drug offending, escape, assault (no imprisonment)
- 2008: sentenced 4 years imprisonment (served 16 months)
- 5 June 2020: assault conviction; 10 months imprisonment from 26 January 2021
- 24 January 2021: child abduction and assault
- Late 2021: sentenced 6 years imprisonment; parole eligibility after 4 years
- 6 years registration under Community Protection Act

## Connection to Sandra Anne Davis and Kenneth William Mabb

Working hypothesis: this Cecil Maurice Mabb is the "Ccyril Mabb" listed in the Thompson genealogy as a son of Sandra Anne Davis and Kenneth William Mabb.

Supporting evidence:
1. The name "Ccyril" in the Thompson genealogy appears to be a transcription error for "Cecil"
2. Named after paternal grandfather Cecil Maurice Mabb (1908-1985), Kenneth's father
3. Born approximately 1979, consistent with Sandra's estimated child-bearing years
4. Mother described as "stolen generation" member with alcohol issues: Sandra Anne Davis was Aboriginal (Noble/Wannamutta line)
5. Queensland connection: family lived in FNQ
6. Father relocating to Tasmania with him: Kenneth William Mabb died 5 May 1996 in Queensland. If the father in the sentencing remarks is Kenneth, the Tasmania relocation at age 5 (approximately 1984) predates Kenneth's death. Alternatively, "father" could refer to a step-father or other figure.

Contradicting evidence:
1. Kenneth William Mabb died in Queensland in 1996 and is buried at Innisfail Cemetery. If Kenneth is the father who moved to Tasmania, the timeline is complex but not impossible (could have returned to QLD before death).
2. The sentencing remarks do not name the parents directly.

Confidence: Speculative. The circumstantial case is strong but no direct documentary link confirms parentage.
