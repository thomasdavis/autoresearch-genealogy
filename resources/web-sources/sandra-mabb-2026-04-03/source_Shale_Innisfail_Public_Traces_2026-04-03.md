# Public Shale Traces in Innisfail

This note captures the strongest public-source trail found for the `Shale` branch connected to Sandra Anne Davis.

## Mojowire Post, 20 February 2013

The Mojowire post `FNQ BAT BITE FEEDS QUEENSLAND BAT CONTROL DEBATE`, published `20 February 2013`, states that:

1. `Nicholas Shale` was bitten by a microbat
2. the family was at `Innisfail` in Far North Queensland
3. Nicholas's mother was named `Sam Shale`

This is a concrete public trace for a Shale family in `Innisfail` in `2013`.
