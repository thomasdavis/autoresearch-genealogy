# CDFHS Pioneers and Settlers Register: Far North Queensland to 1920

Source: Ancestry.com, "Far North Queensland, Australia, Pioneers & Settlers Registers, 1825-1920" [database on-line]. Collection 1950. Lehi, UT: Ancestry.com Operations, Inc. Indexed by Ancestry World Archives Project contributors.

Original data: *Pioneers & Settlers Register Far North Queensland to 1920*. Print Publication, 1 volume. Cairns and District Family History Society Inc., Cairns, QLD, Australia.

URL: https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/1950/

## About This Collection

This database contains registers of pioneers and settlers who came to Far North Queensland between 1825 and 1920. Records were compiled from entries received through descendants of these pioneers. The volume is arranged alphabetically and contains a wide range of information.

Fields always present: Surname, Birth date and place, Parents' names, Spouse's name, Children's names and birth dates, Residence.

Fields sometimes present: Marriage date and location, Occupation, Religion, Death date, Grave location, Date and place of arrival in Australia.

### Patching-Reynolds Connection Confirmed

Charles Patching Jr. (b. 8 Jul 1870, Brighton area originally, d. 21 Apr 1935, Cooktown) married Catherine Reynolds (b. 1873, daughter of Owen Reynolds and Mary Connors) in 1898. Their children's middle names encode the family connections:
- "Conners" from grandmother Mary Connors
- "Owen" from grandfather Owen Reynolds
- "Endeavour" likely from Cooktown's Endeavour River

### Confidence Assessment

- Owen Reynolds family data: **Strong Signal**. This is a compiled register based on descendant submissions, cross-referencing with other known records (death date, family members).
- The register was compiled by CDFHS from information provided by descendants. It is a secondary source that aggregates family knowledge, not a primary record. However, it is valuable for the family structure and birth dates it provides.
- The absence of Owen Reynolds' own parents or birth place in the indexed data is a gap. The original register images may contain additional detail not captured in the Ancestry index.

### Discrepancies to Investigate

1. Owen's birth year: 1845 here vs 1844 in some other sources
2. Two children named "Emily Rose Reynolds" (b. 1875 and 1888): did the first die young?
3. No birth place recorded for Owen Reynolds himself
4. Mary Connors has no standalone entry: her origins remain unknown from this source
5. The register is based on descendant submissions, so the information reflects family tradition rather than primary documentation
