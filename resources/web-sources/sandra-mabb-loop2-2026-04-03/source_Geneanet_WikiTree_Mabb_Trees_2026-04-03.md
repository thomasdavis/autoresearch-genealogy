# Geneanet and WikiTree Mabb Family Tree Extracts

## Geneanet: bmuckleston tree

Brian Muckleston's tree confirms:
- Cecil Maurice Mabb (c. 1909 Hanwell, Middlesex; d. 26 Feb 1985 Mount Isa, QLD)
- Partner: Florence Helen May Craig (1908-1962)
- Children: Kenneth Mabb, Rita Mabb, Mabel Evelyn Gwendoline Mabb

The tree does NOT extend to Kenneth's wife Sandra Anne Davis or any grandchildren. Geneanet blocked direct page fetching (403).

Brian Muckleston is the best contact for extending the tree below Cecil.

## WikiTree: Henry John Mabb (Mabb-54)

Full profile: baptised 20 Nov 1870 Wadhurst, Sussex; d. 11 Jan 1914 QLD. Parents: Richard Mabb and Frances Weaver. Married Susannah Underdown (b. 1875 Matfield, Kent) in 1894, Tunbridge Wells. Occupation: tramway motorman. Ten children confirmed including Cecil Mabb (b. c. 1909 Hanwell, Middlesex).

## WikiTree: Charles Henry Mabb (Mabb-55)

Cecil's brother. Born 1897 Richmond, Surrey; d. 27 May 1938 QLD. Married Rosa Jane Myrtle Somerville (b. 16 Sep 1896 QLD) on 15 Jul 1922. Children:
- Dulcie Rosa Mabb (1923-2015, Geebung/Kawana Waters QLD)
- Henry Albert Mabb (b. 1925, Geebung)

Susannah Underdown's second married name was Felix (remarried after Henry's 1914 death). This explains the Susannah Mabb / David George Felix 1916 marriage.

## WikiTree: Wendy Fullbrook (Mabb-58)

DEAD END for this research. Wendy L. Fullbrook (nee Mabb, also Payne), born 1950s. Mother: Betty Jean Odell (Davison) Mabb (9 Mar 1926-1989, Doncaster, Yorkshire). This is a different Mabb line (Yorkshire, not Queensland). DNA tested on MyHeritage. No connection to Cecil's branch is evident.

## Leslie Victor Mabb

BillionGraves confirms: died 23 August 1978, age 65. Buried Albany Creek Memorial Park, Brisbane. Ancestry records show residences in both Lilley and Kennedy divisions (1930s electoral rolls). Married Heather Crispe. The Kennedy electoral roll placement confirms the FNQ connection (Kennedy covered Cairns area).

Leslie was likely born in Queensland after the 1911 arrival (not on the ship manifest). Born 19 Dec 1912.

## SS Oswestry Grange 1911 Passenger List

Departed London 15 Jul 1911. Passengers: Henry (39), Mrs Mabb (35), Ernest (15), Mabel (16), Charles (14), Elsie (11), Beatrice (8), Fred (5), Stanley (4), Christopher (4), Cecil (2), Phyllis (2 months). Total: 12 (2 parents + 10 children).
