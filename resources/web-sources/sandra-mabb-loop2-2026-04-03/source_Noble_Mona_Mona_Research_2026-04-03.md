# Noble Family at Mona Mona: Expanded Research

## Jack Noble / Wannamutta (c.1855-1935)

ADB biography at ia.anu.edu.au confirms:
- Born Sandy Cape, K'gari (Fraser Island)
- Parents: Jacko Morris and Carie Martin (Butchulla people)
- Recruited age 14 by Constable Thomas King into Queensland Native Mounted Police
- Deployed to Victoria in 1879 to track the Kelly Gang
- Present at the Siege of Glenrowan, 29 June 1880
- Married Alberta Clift (Quandamooka, Stradbroke Island) at Bogimbah mission, early 1900s
- Customarily adopted Alberta's son George, who took the surname Noble
- Removed to Yarrabah 3 October 1902
- George remained at Yarrabah when Jack and Alberta were moved to Myora (1906), then Barambah/Cherbourg (1926, 1928)
- Died 15 January 1935 at Pialba of kidney disease, aged approximately 80
- Buried Polson Cemetery, Pialba

Note: The ADB does not mention George Clifford Noble, Molly, Dorothy, or Mona Mona specifically.

Frontier Conflict database has a dedicated page on Jack Noble's NMP service: frontierconflict.org/jack-noble

## 1914 Removal Record (CIFHS)

The CIFHS Queensland Removals Register 1912-1939 records:
"George Noble and wife Molly and child George" removed from Yarrabah to Mona Mona in 1914.
Reason: "Interfering with dormitary girls" [sic]

This confirms the known facts and adds the detail that child George (later George Gregory Noble, b. 1915) accompanied them.

## CIFHS Marriage Register Entries

### George Gregory Noble and Alice Mona McKenzie, 1935

CPA Marriage Register 1908-1936 records:
- Bride: Alice Mona McKenzie
- Groom: George Gregory Noble
- Year: 1935
- Place: Cairns

Bride's full name now confirmed as Alice Mona McKenzie.

### Annie Noble and William Pitt, 1920

CPA Marriage Register records:
- Bride: Annie Noble
- Groom: William Pitt
- Year: 1920
- Place: Yarrabah

Per Dr Jeremy Hodes's Queensland History blog, after his first wife Mary died, William Pitt married Annie Noble from Yarrabah; they later moved to Mossman before WWII.

Annie is likely an older generation Noble rather than Agnes Norma Loretta Noble (b. 1916), who would have been only 4 in 1920. The exact relationship between Annie Noble and George Clifford Noble needs clarification.

### Mary Ann Noble and Ropeyarn, 1932

- Bride: Mary Ann Noble
- Groom: Ropeyarn
- Year: 1932
- Place: Palm Island

Possible relative of the Mona Mona Nobles. The Palm Island connection may indicate transfer from another mission.

## Tindale Genealogies (1938)

Norman Tindale visited Mona Mona in 1938 and recorded genealogies. The State Library of Queensland holds copies. Access is restricted to direct descendants or those with community/family approval. The Noble family would almost certainly appear in these records given their residence from 1914 onward.

Contact: SLQ directly for descendant access.

Also held at AIATSIS: aiatsis.gov.au/family-history/family-history-sources/tindale-genealogies
