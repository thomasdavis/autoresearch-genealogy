# Electoral Roll and Public Record Search: Kenneth and Sandra Mabb

### Cecil Maurice Mabb (Kenneth's father)
- QLD BDM death registration 1985/52244: born 1908, died 26 February 1985. Parents: Henry Mabb and Susan Underdown.
- Buried at Mount Isa Sunset Lawn Cemetery, Plot 1, Grave #1179, interred 4 March 1985, age 75.
- Confirms Cecil was in Mount Isa at death.

### Leslie Victor Mabb (Kenneth's uncle)
- Ancestry records show residences in both Lilley and Kennedy divisions, Queensland (1930s electoral rolls). Married Heather Crispe.
