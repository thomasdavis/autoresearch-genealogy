# Yarrabah Baptism Register: Key Entries for Davis/Rosie/Julia/Nellie Research

Full page downloaded to: resources/web-sources/yarrabah_baptisms_full.html

## FREDERICK BRACKENRIDGE (1899)

Baptised: **21 May 1899** (Whitsunday)
Christian Name: **Frederick**
Father: **George Brackenridge**
Mother: **Rosie Brackenridge**
Abode: **Mossman**

**Significance**: Confirms Rosie was using surname "Brackenridge" and living at Mossman in 1899. George Brackenridge is listed as father. This is Frederick (born c.1894 per delayed registration).

## JULIA H/C (1906)

Baptised: **27 May 1906**
Christian Name: **Julia H/C**
Age: **About 11 years old**
Father: (blank)
Mother: (blank)
Abode: **Yarrabah**

**Significance**: Julia baptised at Yarrabah 1906, age ~11, NO PARENTS LISTED. This matches the 1905 removal from Rossville as orphan age 9. If age 11 in 1906, born c.1895.

## KEY FINDINGS

1. **Nellie's husband identified**: Jhinna/Jena Murgha = John Barlow (baptised Yarrabah 1894/1895). He was a carpenter, later fireman. Full blood Aboriginal.

2. **Nellie confirmed as "Nellie Watkin H/C"** - half caste, confirming European father (Owen Reynolds).

3. **Stafford Murgha born 4 Jan 1919** at Yarrabah - NOT 1887 as in Allan Brackenridge's tree. The 1887 date in the tree is wrong, or it's a different Stafford.

4. **Rosie Shoman H/C** adult baptism Nov 1911, "from Mareeba" - if this is our Rosie, she was ALIVE in 1911, contradicting the 1904 death.

5. **Julia and Victor Leftwich** had children at Yarrabah from 1914 onwards (Victor Frederick, Bessie Rosina, Laurence Eddie, Ismay Julia, Cecil Copeland).

6. **Julia's 1906 baptism lists NO PARENTS** - consistent with being an orphan when she arrived.

7. **Nellie's children with Jhinna Murgha**: Richard (1917), Stafford (1919), Raymond (1921), Alexander (1923), Eric (1925, died 1926).
