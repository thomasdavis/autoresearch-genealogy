# CIFHS Key Records: Connecting Julia, Nellie, Noble, Murgha, and Molloy

## Julia Nunn Marriage (Yarrabah Marriages 1901-1916)

- Date: **15 February 1912** at St Alban's Church, Yarrabah
- Minister: W G Ivens, Church of England
- Bride: **Julia Nunn**, of Yarrabah, Spinster, Born at Romeo, Age 18
- Father: **Davis**
- Mother: **Rosie Rosie**
- **WITNESSES: George Noble and Nellie**

**CRITICAL**: George Noble AND Nellie witnessed Julia's marriage. This proves:
1. Nellie was at Yarrabah in Feb 1912 and close to Julia (wedding witness)
2. George Noble was connected to this family before marrying into the Davis line
3. The Noble-Davis connection predates the Charles Davis-Dorothy Noble marriage

## George Noble Marriage (Yarrabah Marriages)

- Date: **11 May 1911** at St Alban's Church, Yarrabah
- Minister: W G Ivens, Church of England
- Groom: **George Noble**
- Bride: **Molly Abreson**
- Witnesses included George Noble and Molly Abreson

**Note**: "Molly Abreson" may be the same person later known as "Molly Hewison" or "Molly Noble" - Edgar's wife Caroline Molloy's mother-in-law through the Noble marriage.

## George Noble Removal (Queensland Removals 1912-1939)

- **George Noble and wife Molly** removed from **Yarrabah to Mona Mona**
- Reason: **"Interfering with dormitory girls"**
- Also: child George removed Yarrabah to Mona Mona

## Nellie Murgha Marriage Application (Card Index 1934-1941)

- Applied: **5 May 1937** at Cloncurry, Legal
- HUSBAND: **Dan Owens**, Born **1894**, Caste **H/C**
- WIFE: **Nellie Murgha**, Born **1897**, Caste **H/C**
- Previous Marriage: To Unnamed (i.e., Jhinna Murgha)
- **MARRIED 1941**

**Note**: Nellie's birth year listed as **1897** here, not 1900-1901. If born 1897, she was 8 at the 1905 removal (listed as 4.5) - a significant discrepancy. OR the 1897 date is approximate.

## KEY CONNECTIONS REVEALED

### 1. George Noble → Julia → Nellie → Edgar
George Noble witnessed Julia's 1912 wedding. Nellie also witnessed it. George Noble later married Molly (Abreson/Hewison). Their daughter Dorothy Noble married Charles Joseph Davis (Edgar's son). So George Noble was connected to Rosie's family BEFORE his daughter married into Edgar's family.

### 2. George Noble → Mona Mona → Willie Molloy
George Noble was removed from Yarrabah to Mona Mona. Willie Molloy was also at Mona Mona. Caroline Rose Molloy was Edgar's wife. Was Willie Molloy related to Caroline? Did the Noble-Molloy connection at Mona Mona lead to the Noble-Davis marriage?

### 3. Yarrabah as Meeting Point
All these families converged at Yarrabah:
- Edgar Davis (baptised 1896, married 1909, son baptised 1910)
- Julia Nunn (baptised 1906, married 1912)
- Nellie Watkin (baptised 1906, married Jhinna Murgha)
- George Noble (married 1911, witnessed Julia's 1912 wedding)
- Victor Leftwich (married Julia 1912)
- Jhinna Murgha (WW1 census, married Nellie)

### 4. The Noble-Julia Connection Strengthens Julia = Rosie's Daughter
George Noble witnessing Julia's wedding means he knew Julia's family. He later became connected to Edgar's family through the Noble-Davis marriage. This is NOT a coincidence - the Noble family was embedded in the same Yarrabah community as Rosie's children (Edgar, Julia, Nellie). The families knew each other.
