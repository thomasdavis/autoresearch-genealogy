## Irish Origin Analysis

The surnames in the deep ancestry suggest a **Leinster/Midlands Ireland** origin:
- **Reynolds**: Common throughout Ireland, particularly Leitrim/Roscommon (Connacht border) and Leinster
- **Dwyer/O'Dwyer**: Stronghold in Tipperary (Munster) and surrounding Leinster counties
- **Mulhall**: Concentrated in Laois (Queen's County) and Offaly (King's County) in the Irish Midlands
- **Carmichael**: Scottish origin, common in Ulster and northern Leinster
- **Connolly**: Very common throughout Ireland, especially Connacht and Leinster

This combination suggests the Reynolds family originated in the **Leinster/Connacht border region** of Ireland, consistent with the known Irish migration patterns to Far North Queensland in the 1860s-1880s.

## Significance

This is the first time the Davis/Brackenridge family has known about Owen Reynolds as their ancestor. The ThruLines confirmation, combined with 127-149 cM DNA matches to Owen's legitimate descendants (Rawlinson/Patching family through Catherine Reynolds), provides strong evidence for this lineage.

The Reynolds family were Irish immigrants who arrived in Far North Queensland in the early 1880s. Five brothers (Owen, James, Patrick, Thomas, William) all settled in the Cooktown/Port Douglas/Daintree corridor. Owen ran the Family Hotel at Cooktown's Two Mile from 1900-1913+.
