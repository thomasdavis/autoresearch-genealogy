## "Fourmile" Surname

Fourmile is a Gimuy (Cairns) Aboriginal surname associated with Yarrabah mission descendants. The name likely derives from "Four Mile Beach" or a four-mile distance marker on the road between Cairns and Yarrabah. The Fourmile family is prominent in Cairns Aboriginal community and culture.
