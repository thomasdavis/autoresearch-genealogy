# Julia Nunn/Leftwich: DNA Match Search and Analysis

## Who Is Julia?

- Born c.1894 at **Romeo** (near Rossville/Bloomfield)
- Father: **"Davis"** (per 1912 marriage record)
- Mother: **"Rosie Rosie"** (per 1912 marriage record)
- Removed from Rossville 1905 as orphan, age 9, with sister Nellie (age 4.5)
- Foster family: **Nunn** ("Julia said the Nunn family looked after her after her mother died")
- Married **Victor Leftwich** at Yarrabah, 15 Feb 1912
- Died 1986 (reg 1986/52864)
- Fred Leftwich (grandson) confirmed "Nellie Watkins was Julia's sister"

## Julia's Father: "Davis"

Julia's 1912 Yarrabah marriage record names her father as **"Davis"** - a surname, not a first name. This suggests:

1. A European or non-Aboriginal man named Davis was Julia's biological father
2. OR the surname was adopted from the **Davis Hill** household at Bloomfield
3. OR "Davis" was assigned by mission staff based on association

### Davis Hill Connection
- **Davis Hill** is a real place at Bloomfield, near Rossville and Romeo
- Herbert Olufson (OH55/10, 1990s): "the Davis's from Davis Hill" and "Martha Davis from Davis Hill"
- No named older male "George Davis" has been identified at Davis Hill
- The Davis Hill household has no proven direct connection to Edgar, Julia, Nellie, or Yarrabah
- But it's in exactly the right location (Romeo/Rossville/Bloomfield area)

### Filipino Connection
Charles Joseph Davis's 1938 exemption record describes Edgar's parents as **"H/C Phillipino, H/C Aborigine"**. If the "Davis" man was Filipino:
- Filipino men worked in sugar/pearl-shell in FNQ in the 1880s-1890s
- "Filipino" was sometimes used as a catch-all for Asian/Pacific Islander men
- A Filipino man at Davis Hill/Bloomfield could be Julia's father
- This would explain the "H/C Phillipino" classification (though it's on Edgar's son's record, not Julia's)
