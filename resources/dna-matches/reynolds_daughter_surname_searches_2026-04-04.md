## Key Finding

The surname searches for Owen's daughters' married names (Meldrum, Penny, Lee, Potter) and direct Reynolds surname have NOT produced any strong new Reynolds-line matches beyond what was already known (Rawlinson/Patching at 127-149 cM).

All Reynolds-surname matches either:
1. Connect through the Aboriginal/Pitt side (Danielle Reynolds, R.S.)
2. Are from different Reynolds lines entirely (stephen reynolds with Danish Reinholdt origin)
3. Are part of unidentified European clusters (Lori Denton/T.H. Mohn-Bloomer cluster)

The strongest evidence for the Owen Reynolds connection remains the Rawlinson/Patching cluster (Kathryn Rawlinson 149 cM, Scorpio88 127 cM), which connects through Catherine Reynolds specifically.

This pattern is expected: Owen's children with Mary Connors settled in Far North Queensland. Their descendants may not have taken Ancestry DNA tests in large numbers. The Rawlinson/Patching family are the exception, having tested, and they provide the critical link.
