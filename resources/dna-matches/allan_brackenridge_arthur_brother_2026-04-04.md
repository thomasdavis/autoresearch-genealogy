## Significance

1. **Confirms Arthur Brackenridge survived to adulthood** and had children. The delayed birth registration (1906/O/924) listed four of Rosie's children: Edgar (1890), Joseph (1892), Frederick (1894), Arthur (1897). We now know Arthur's son was Allan Henry Leonard Brackenridge (1925-2007).

2. **Arthur married Florence Gordon** - "Gordon" is a common FNQ surname, both Aboriginal and European.

3. **Kawoot** surname (Aboriginal) appears through Myra Kawoot/Cole who married into the family.

4. The **9 cM** shared between Lisa and Allan is consistent with Edgar and Arthur being half-siblings (same mother Rosie, possibly different fathers) or full siblings (if the same father). If Owen Reynolds fathered both Edgar and Arthur, they would be full siblings, and Lisa and Allan would be expected to share more DNA. The low 9 cM suggests either:
   - They are half-siblings (different fathers), or
   - Random DNA inheritance resulted in low sharing at this generational distance

5. The 1915-1916 War Census listed **Fred Braikenridge** at Port Douglas on the same page as Rosie Honalle and Nellie. Now we also have Arthur's line documented through his son Allan.
