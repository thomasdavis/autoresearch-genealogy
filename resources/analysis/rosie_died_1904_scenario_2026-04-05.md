# Scenario: Rosie (Edgar's Mother) Died in 1904

## The Case FOR This Scenario

### 1. Physical Burial Record Exists
Pioneer Cemetery, Cairns has a confirmed burial:
- **Plot C449**
- **"ROSIE, Unknown"**
- **Buried 8 April 1904**
- **Mixed Denominations section**

This is a REAL record in the cemetery database. "ROSIE, Unknown" means no surname was recorded - consistent with an Aboriginal woman. The burial is 9 days after the Thompson genealogy's death date of 30 March 1904.

### 2. Family Knowledge (Multiple Sources)
- **Sanita-Ann Shale's tree** (1,075 people, most detailed): "Rosie Reynolds, died 30 March 1904, buried Pioneer Cemetery, Cairns"
- **Thompson genealogy**: "Rosie is buried at the Pioneer cemetery in Cairns. The graveside is unmarked but the wall details show her death in 1904, age 35."
- **Ancestry ThruLines**: Lists Rosie Reynolds' death as 1904
- This is FAMILY KNOWLEDGE passed down through the Davis family. Multiple tree builders independently record 1904.

### 3. 1905 Protector Report: "Orphans"
The 1905 Chief Protector report refers to Julia and Nellie as connected to Rosie's family in a way suggesting Rosie was no longer alive. The term "orphans" appears in context.

### 4. Rosie's Age at Death (If Born c.1869)
- Died March 1904, age 35 = born c.1869
- This MATCHES the 1942 indigence application (age 73 = born 1869) and 1964 death cert (age 95 = born 1869)
- BUT WAIT: if the 1942/1964 Rosie was born 1869 AND the 1904 Rosie was born 1869, they have the same birth year
- This could mean: (a) same woman (1904 date wrong), or (b) TWO women born around the same time in the same area

### 5. No BDM Death Registration
No "Rosie" death (any surname) appears in the 1904 Queensland BDM index except "Rosie Lewis" (European). Aboriginal deaths were frequently unregistered in this period.

### 6. Naming Pattern: Nellie
- Rosie's 1964 death certificate says her mother was **"Nellie"**
- Rosie named her daughter **"Nellie"** (born c.1900)
- This is a common pattern: naming a child after one's own mother
- If true, Rosie's mother Nellie was likely born in the 1840s-1850s, Kuku Yalanji country

## The Brackenridge Question

### Were Joseph, Frederick, and Arthur Actually Rosie's Children?

**Arguments they WERE Rosie's:**
- Registered on the same delayed form as Edgar (1906/O/924)
- All listed under "George Brackenridge" as father and "Rosie" as mother
- Frederick baptised at Yarrabah in 1899 with mother "Rosie Brackenridge, abode Mossman"

**Arguments they may NOT have been Rosie's:**
- The delayed registration was filed in 1906, after Rosie's death (if she died 1904)
- Someone else filled in the form and may have lumped children together
- "Brackenridge" was a surname associated with Yarrabah - multiple children there had this name
- The DNA evidence: Allan Brackenridge (Arthur's descendant) shares only 9 cM with Lisa; Josephine Alderman (Frederick's descendant) shares 17 cM. These are LOW for half-siblings' grandchildren, but within range
- If Joseph/Frederick/Arthur had a DIFFERENT mother than Edgar, the DNA would be even lower (they'd be half-siblings through father only, if George Brackenridge was their father and Edgar's step-father)

### The DNA Test
If Joseph, Frederick, and Arthur were Rosie's children (same mother as Edgar), their descendants should share:
- ~50% of Rosie's DNA (as her children)
- Plus any shared DNA from the father (George Brackenridge vs Owen Reynolds)

Edgar's DNA to Lisa: ~12.5% (great-grandfather)
Arthur's DNA to Lisa (if same mother Rosie, different father): ~6.25% from Rosie's side only
At the 2nd cousin 1x removed level: expected ~3.1% = ~25-50 cM

Allan Brackenridge (Arthur's grandchild): 9 cM with Lisa
Josephine Alderman (Frederick's grandchild): 17 cM with Lisa

These are LOW but within the statistical range for this relationship. The low values could indicate:
1. Random inheritance (possible at this distance)
2. Joseph/Frederick/Arthur share ONLY a mother with Edgar (not a father) = half-siblings
3. OR they share a DIFFERENT mother entirely and only connect through the Yarrabah community

## The "Rosie Hippie/Julian" (d. 1964) Question

If our Rosie died in 1904, then **Rosie Hippie/Homarlee Epi/Julian** who died in 1964 is a DIFFERENT woman.

**Why this could work:**
- The 1942 indigence form says "no sons or daughters" - if she was Edgar's mother, why would she say that?
- The ages given in 1948 (60) and 1949 (57) don't match someone born 1869 - but they DO match someone born ~1888-1892
- "Rosie Hippie" could be a woman who married Willie Api independently, not connected to Edgar
- Father "Mick" and mother "Nellie" on the 1964 death cert could be her OWN parents, not Edgar's grandmother's parents

**Problems with this:**
- The 1915 War Census has "Rosie Honalle" with Fred Braikenridge at Port Douglas. If our Rosie died 1904, why is Fred with a "Rosie"?
  - POSSIBLE ANSWER: Fred is listed in the SAME BLOCK as Rosie Honalle, but they may not be family. The War Census lists all Aboriginal people in a district together. Fred lived at Port Douglas; so did many other Rosies.
- The 1942 form links "Rosie Hippie" to "Rosie Homarlee Epi" - "Homarlee" is very close to "Homalee" (Jimmy Homalee's surname). If this is a different woman, how does she have the same SSI husband's name?
  - POSSIBLE ANSWER: Maybe "Homalee/Homarlee" was a place name or clan name, not specifically from Jimmy Homalee. OR: Maybe this Rosie was Jimmy Homalee's SECOND wife after our Rosie died in 1904.

## The "Second Wife" Theory

**What if Jimmy Homalee married ANOTHER Rosie after our Rosie died?**

Timeline:
1. Our Rosie married Jimmy Homalee (pre-1901)
2. Our Rosie died March 1904
3. Jimmy Homalee then partnered with ANOTHER Aboriginal woman, also called Rosie
4. This second Rosie took the name "Rosie Homalee/Homarlee"
5. Jimmy Homalee dies (pre-1905 - the protector report says "late husband")
6. Second Rosie then partners with Willie Api/Epi
7. Second Rosie becomes "Rosie Hippie/Epi"
8. Second Rosie marries Paddy Julian in 1949
9. Second Rosie dies 1964 as "Rosie Julian"

**Problem**: The 1905 Protector report says "Rosie Homalee of Saltwater Ck, Port Douglas" with late SSI husband and "a son". If our Rosie died in 1904 and Jimmy Homalee also died before 1905, when did this second Rosie marry Jimmy? The timeline is very tight (1904-1905 = only 1 year).
