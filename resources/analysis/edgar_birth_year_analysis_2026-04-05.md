## The Newspaper Article (May 1908)

Edgar Davis wrote **"A Little About Yarrabah"** published 22 May 1908 in a Trove-indexed newspaper. He was described as **literate, articulate, and engaging with mission intellectual life**.

This was 7 months before his marriage in October 1909.

| If Born | Age at Article (May 1908) | Assessment |
|---------|--------------------------|------------|
| 1883 | **25** | Mature adult, very plausible for articulate writing |
| 1887 | **21** | Young adult, plausible |
| 1889 | **19** | Possible but young for the described style |
| 1890 | **18** | Possible but very young |

## The 1896 Baptism Problem

Edgar was baptised at Yarrabah on **19 January 1896**, age "approximately 7".

| If Born | Actual Age at Baptism | Error in Estimate |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------|
| 1883 | **13** | Guessed 6 years too young |
| 1887 | **9** | Guessed 2 years too young |
| 1889 | **7** | Exact match |
| 1890 | **6** | Guessed 1 year too old |

A 6-year error (if born 1883) sounds large, but missionaries frequently misjudged Aboriginal children's ages. A 13-year-old who was small or malnourished could be estimated as 7. However, the difference between a 7-year-old and a 13-year-old is usually obvious.

**This is the strongest argument against 1883.** A 13-year-old being estimated as 7 is a big miss.

## The Marriage Age Problem

Edgar married on 9 October 1909, age stated as **22 years** = born c.1887.

But:
- Edgar may not have known his exact birth year
- The mission may have recorded his age based on the 1896 baptism estimate (age 7 in 1896 = born 1889, so age 20 in 1909, close to 22)
- Aboriginal people in this era often had approximate ages in records

## The Death Certificate Problem

Edgar died 1977. Multiple family trees say he was born **1883** and died at age **94**.

If true:
- He was 13 at the 1896 baptism (estimated as 7)
- He was 22 at the 1908 newspaper article (makes sense)
- He was 26 at marriage (but said 22)
- He was 94 at death

The 4-year discrepancy at marriage (said 22, was actually 26) is plausible if Edgar genuinely didn't know his birth year and used the mission's estimate.
