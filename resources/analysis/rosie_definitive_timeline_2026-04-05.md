# Rosie: Definitive Timeline and Source Evaluation

## THE IDENTITY QUESTION

There are multiple women named "Rosie" in the FNQ records. The key question: which records belong to OUR Rosie (Edgar's mother)?

### Confirmed Identity Chain
Our Rosie = the mother on Edgar's 1906 delayed birth registration (1906/O/924) = "Rosie" at McIvor River.

The following alias chain is PROBABLE but not fully proven:
**Rosie (birth cert) = Rosie Homalee/Homarlee = Rosie Honalle = Rosie Hippie/Hippi = Rosie Epi = Rosie Julian**

Evidence for the chain:
- 1942 indigence application links "Rosie Hippie" to "Rosie Homarlee Epi" (same form, same person)
- Port Douglas 1915 War Census has "Rosie Honalle" with "Fred Braikenridge" (Edgar's brother) in the same block
- "Rosie Julian" death cert (1964) lists father "Mick", consistent with someone from Mareeba/Tablelands area
- All lived in Mossman/Port Douglas/Cooktown corridor

### THE 1904 DEATH PROBLEM

**Sanita-Ann Shale's tree says**: Rosie Reynolds, died 30 March 1904, buried Pioneer Cemetery, Cairns
**BUT**: 1942 indigence application, 1948 Mt Carbine list, 1949 exemption, and 1964 death cert all show a Rosie Hippie/Julian alive decades later.

**Possible explanations:**
1. The 1904 death is a DIFFERENT "Rosie Reynolds" (there were many Rosies)
2. The 1904 date in the family tree is WRONG (a different event was confused with death)
3. There really were two Rosies in Owen Reynolds' life

**Best estimate: c.1869** (two independent sources agree: 1942 application AND 1964 death cert)

**The age discrepancies** (1948 saying 60, 1949 saying 57) are common in Aboriginal records where ages were estimated by officials who often guessed low.

## DNA EVIDENCE FOR ROSIE'S ANCESTRY

### What Lisa's DNA tells us about Rosie:
- Lisa is 25% Aboriginal - but this comes from MULTIPLE Aboriginal ancestors (Rosie, plus Noble/Roberts/Kleft Cliff/Nughi lines through other branches)
- Rosie's Aboriginal DNA contribution to Lisa: approximately 3-6% (as a 2nd great-grandmother contributing ~6.25% total, half of which is Aboriginal if Rosie was full blood = ~3.1%)
- The "unidentified European" cluster (Drake/Whiteley/ChrisVickey) does NOT share DNA with Hezron Murgha (Nellie's line), ruling out these matches connecting through a European father of Rosie

### Government classifications of Rosie:
- **"F" (Full Blood)**: 1910 Cooktown death register, 1916 Port Douglas death register
- **"Aboriginal"**: All Protector records consistently
- **"Half-castes" (Rosie and Jeanie)**: ONE record describes them as half-castes being removed to Yarrabah
- **"F/B or H/C Kanaka"**: 1948 Mt Carbine list (ambiguous: full blood or half-caste kanaka)

### Assessment:
Rosie was most likely **full or predominantly Aboriginal**. The single "half-caste" reference may be an error, or may reflect that officials sometimes used the term loosely. The "F" classifications in two separate death registers are more reliable as they were official administrative categories.

If Rosie had a European father, we would expect the Drake/Whiteley DNA sub-group to also share DNA with Nellie's descendants (Hezron Murgha), but they DO NOT. This suggests Rosie's parents were both Aboriginal.
