# Julia Nunn / Leftwich: Complete Profile

## Identity

**Julia Nunn** (foster name) → **Julia Leftwich** (married name)

- Born: **1894** (confirmed on death record), at **Romeo** (near Rossville/Bloomfield)
- Julia herself said: born "around the Bloomfield area"
- Father: **"Davis"** (1912 marriage record)
- Mother: **"Rosie Rosie"** (1912 marriage record)
- Clan: **Kuku Yalanji** (Eastern Kuku Yalanji)
- Died: **27 April 1986**, reg 1986/52864, father/parent listed as "- Nunn"

## Julia's Own Story (from "Reaching Back" oral history, published 1989, ed. Judy Thomson, Aboriginal Studies Press)

- Her mother died → the **Nunn family at Nunnville/Rossville** looked after her
- She was "about 16" when police took her away from the Cooktown side (age conflict with 1905 removal at age 9)
- She was put in the dormitory at about 16
- Fred Leftwich (her son): "Nellie Watkins was Julia's sister"

## Nunnville / Nunn Family

- **William Nunn** (c.1820-1905) operated **Nunnville Station**, Cook District
- **Nunn's Forks**: where Wallaby Creek meets Annan River near Helenvale - between Rossville and Cooktown
- Julia took the Nunn surname as a foster child after her mother died
- The 1986 death record lists father/parent as "- Nunn" (foster surname persisted to death)

## Marriage

- Married **Victor Alfred Leftwich** (also "Beengho Victor Alfred Leftwich")
- **15 February 1912** at **St Alban's Church, Yarrabah**
- Victor born: **4 March 1893, Fraser Island, Queensland**
- Victor died: **5 July 1977, Cairns** (or 7 May 1977 per another source)
- Marriage record: father "Davis", mother "Rosie Rosie"
- BDM marriage reg: **2010/N/4576**

## The "Davis" Father Question

Julia's 1912 marriage record names her father as **"Davis"**. This is NOT Edgar (who would be her half-brother). This is her ACTUAL father - a man with the surname Davis.

### Possible identities of "Davis":
1. **A man from Davis Hill, Bloomfield** - oral history confirms "the Davis's from Davis Hill" and "Martha Davis from Davis Hill" at Bloomfield (OH55/10 Herbert Olufson)
2. **A Filipino settler** - Charles Joseph Davis's 1938 exemption says Edgar's parents were "H/C Phillipino, H/C Aborigine." Filipino "Manilamen" worked in FNQ pearling from 1880s.
3. **A European settler** working at Bloomfield/Romeo area
4. **A surname assigned from the Davis Hill locality** rather than a biological father

### Davis Hill at Bloomfield
- Real place confirmed in oral histories (OH55/01 Frank O'Rourke, OH55/10 Herbert Olufson)
- "Martha Davis from Davis Hill" is the only named individual
- No male Davis identified at Davis Hill
- Located on south side of Bloomfield River mouth
- Near Romeo (Julia's birthplace) and Rossville (where Julia and Nellie were removed)

## Romeo Locality

Julia's birthplace "Romeo" is near Rossville, Shipton's Flat, and Bloomfield. People from "Shipton's Flat, Romeo, and Rossville" gathered together at Nunnville. No detailed map location found online - may be a very small historical place name.

- Julia's descendants share DNA with Lisa through Rosie (common grandmother)

- Julia's descendants would NOT match Kathryn Rawlinson (different father from Owen Reynolds)

**BUT**: We cannot confirm this without a DNA test from a Leftwich descendant.

If Julia and Nellie were FULL sisters (same father), then Julia's father "Davis" = Owen Reynolds under a different name, and the theory changes entirely.
