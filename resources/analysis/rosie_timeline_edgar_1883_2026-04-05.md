## Owen Reynolds' Two Periods with Rosie

If Edgar born 1883 and Nellie born c.1899-1901, Owen fathered children with Rosie in TWO separate periods:

**Period 1 (c.1882-1885)**: Owen is 37-40, running the Family Hotel at Cooktown. Rosie is 13-16. Aboriginal camp at Two Mile is near Owen's hotel. Owen fathers Edgar.

**Period 2 (c.1899-1901)**: Owen is 54-56. His last child with Mary Connors was born 1891 (James Connors Reynolds). Owen is still at Cooktown. Rosie is now 30-32 and has been with George Brackenridge. Owen fathers Nellie.

**Why two periods?** Owen may have had an ongoing relationship with Rosie, or they reconnected after Rosie's relationship with George Brackenridge. The ~16 year gap between Edgar and Nellie is large but Owen's other children with Mary Connors span 1872-1891 (19 years), so he was clearly fertile over a long period.
