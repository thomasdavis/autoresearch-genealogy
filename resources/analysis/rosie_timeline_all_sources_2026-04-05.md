### The Two-Rosie Problem
There may be TWO different Rosies being conflated:
1. **"Rosie Reynolds" (d. ~1904)** - the mother of Edgar's birth certificate children (1890-1897), partner of "George Brackenridge" 
2. **"Rosie Julian/Hippie/Epi" (d. 1964)** - the woman who lived to 95, married Paddy Julian, Willie Api

OR they are the same woman and the 1904 date is wrong.

## OPEN QUESTIONS

1. **Is the 1904 death (Rosie Reynolds, age 35) a different Rosie?** Sanita-Ann's tree says d.1904, but death cert says d.1964 age 95.
2. **Who was "George Brackenridge"?** DNA says Owen Reynolds was the father, but who was the man called George Brackenridge? Was this Owen using a false name? Was it a different man? Or was Brackenridge a real person who raised the children?
3. **Who was Mick (father)?** Aboriginal man, no surname. Possibly Mick Gray (Tommy Gray's father too?).
4. **Who was Nellie (mother)?** Aboriginal woman, no surname. Rosie's mother.
5. **Was Rosie "half-caste"?** The Drake/Whiteley sub-group does NOT share DNA with Nellie's line, ruling out a European father for Rosie as their connection point. However, some records describe Edgar's children as "1/4 caste" which would imply Rosie was "half-caste" (one European parent).
6. **The "1/4 caste" problem**: If Edgar was "1/4 caste" (one source says "Robert Eldon, 1/4C"), this implies Rosie was "1/2 caste" (one white parent, one Aboriginal). But if Rosie's father Mick was Aboriginal, who was the European? Could "Mick" have been a European?
