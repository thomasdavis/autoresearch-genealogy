Edgar Father Candidate Ranking: Reynolds Nephews

This page ranks the Reynolds nephews only, with Owen Reynolds shown as the baseline comparison. The three Edgar anchors are the same ones already in use in the project: 1887 from the 1909 marriage age 22, 1890 from the delayed registration, and 1904 from the baptism-age reading. Earlier anchors are weighted more heavily than the 1904 model.
