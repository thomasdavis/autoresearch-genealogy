## DNA Clustering Evidence

**QUESTIONABLE**: Clustering evidence is indirect and depends on sample sizes and inheritance patterns.

### Evidence Suggesting Rosie Was Full Aboriginal:
1. **Government classifications**: Classified "F" (Full Blood) in 1910 and 1916 official registers
2. **No European DNA sub-group connects through Rosie**: The Drake/Whiteley/ChrisVickey sub-group (19 matches, 24-39 cM) shares DNA with Edgar's descendants but NOT with Hezron Murgha (Nellie's descendants). If Rosie had a European father, his descendants would share DNA with BOTH lines.
3. **Parents listed as "Mick" and "Nellie"**: Both Aboriginal names, no European surnames on 1964 death cert
4. **Kunjandji clan, Eastern Kuku Yalanji**: Identified with a specific Aboriginal language group

### Evidence That Rosie COULD Have Had European Ancestry (QUESTIONABLE):
1. **"Half-castes" label**: ONE record describes "Rosie and Jeanie" as "Half-castes, ROs to Yarrabah" - but this could be an administrative error or refer to a different Rosie
2. **"F/B or H/C Kanaka"**: 1948 Mt Carbine list labels Rosie as "F/B or H/C Kanaka" - ambiguous between full blood and half-caste
3. **Some children described as "1/4 caste"**: "Rosie Shoreman 1/4C" (1910 Yarrabah) - but this is PROBABLY a different Rosie
4. **The "McIvor" alias**: McIvor is a Scottish surname used by some Aboriginal people in FNQ. Could indicate a Scottish ancestor, OR could just be a place name (McIvor River near Cooktown)
5. **Owen Reynolds fathered her children**: Rosie had relationships with European men, so her own parents could also have included a European

### Why the Clustering Evidence Is QUESTIONABLE:
- At 8-40 cM, matches could be identical by state (false positives) rather than identical by descent
- The absence of the Drake/Whiteley group in Hezron's shared matches could be due to random inheritance (they might share DNA through Rosie but didn't inherit the specific segments)
- Sample size is small (only Hezron and a few Murgha/Fourmile matches represent Nellie's line)
- We don't know how many of the "unidentified European" matches connect through Molloy/Roberts vs Noble/Hewison vs some other ancestor
