# Owen Reynolds + Rosie: Parallel Timeline with Locations

## Owen's Location History

Owen arrived in North Queensland in the 1870s as a bullock driver on the Palmer River goldfields road. He settled at Cooktown and ran the Family Hotel at the Two Mile from 1900-1913+.

### Owen's Key Dates
- **1845**: Born (Ireland, Laois/Offaly area)
- **1862**: Married Anne Fox, Brisbane (age 17). Anne disappears from records.
- **1870s**: Arrived Far North Queensland. Bullock driver, Cooktown-Laura-Palmer River route.
- **1872**: First child with Mary Connors (Annie). Owen is 27.
- **1872-1891**: 10 children with Mary Connors over 19 years. All born Cook district.
- **1891**: Last child with Mary Connors (James Connors Reynolds). Owen is 46.
- **1900-1913+**: Licensed publican, Family Hotel, Two Mile, Cooktown. 29 gazette entries.
- **1907**: Son Owen Daniel marries Elizabeth Aitken Cannon. Family starts relocating to Townsville.
- **1913**: Last recorded hotel licence entry.
- **1929**: Dies 2 May 1929, age 84, Cairns District Hospital. Buried Cooktown.

## Edgar and Nellie at Yarrabah: Separated Siblings

**This is the tragedy**: Edgar and Nellie were both Owen Reynolds' children by Rosie, but they likely never knew they were full siblings.

### Edgar's Path
- Born c.1883, Cooktown area
- Raised by Rosie (and possibly George Brackenridge)
- By age ~15-20 (c.1898-1903), possibly at or near Yarrabah
- Rosie dies 1904, Edgar is ~21
- Edgar takes surname "Davis" (not Brackenridge, not Reynolds)
- c.1910: Partners with Caroline Rose Molloy/Roberts
- Lives as an Aboriginal man in the Mossman/Cairns corridor
- Dies 1977 in Mossman area, age ~94

### Nellie's Path
- Born c.1899-1901, Cooktown/Port Douglas area
- Named after Rosie's mother "Nellie"
- Rosie dies 1904, Nellie is only ~3-5 years old
- Sent to Yarrabah Mission (orphaned)
- Grows up at Yarrabah
- 1915: Listed on War Census at Port Douglas (age ~14-16) with "Rosie Honalle" (a different Rosie? or her name is associated with this Rosie)
- Married Daniel Owens (1941)
- Had children including the Murgha line (Hezron Murgha, 93 cM with Lisa)

### Why They May Not Have Known Each Other
1. **Age gap**: Edgar was ~16-18 years older than Nellie. By the time Nellie was old enough to remember, Edgar may have already left.
2. **Different surnames**: Edgar used "Davis" or "Brackenridge/Braikenridge". Nellie used other names.
3. **Yarrabah mixing**: Yarrabah was a mission where hundreds of Aboriginal people from different areas were brought together. Children were often separated from family.
4. **No recorded father**: Neither Edgar nor Nellie had Owen Reynolds listed as their father. Edgar's cert says "George Brackenridge". Nellie has no birth registration at all.
5. **Rosie's early death**: If Rosie died in 1904, the person who knew both children were Owen's was gone. No one else may have known.

### The DNA Connection They Never Knew
- Edgar's great-granddaughter Lisa Davis shares DNA with Nellie's grandson Hezron Murgha (93 cM)
- Hezron shares 98 cM with Kathryn Rawlinson (Owen's legitimate great-great-granddaughter through Catherine)
- This DNA proves Edgar and Nellie had the SAME FATHER: Owen Reynolds
- But Edgar and Nellie themselves probably never knew this

## Owen's Opportunity and Proximity

### Period 1 (c.1882-1883): Edgar Conceived
- Owen is 37-38, an established bullock driver on the Cooktown-Laura route
- The McIvor River (Rosie's birthplace) is on this route, ~50km south of Cooktown
- Rosie is 13-14, living in the McIvor River / Cooktown area
- Aboriginal women and girls were frequently exploited by European men on the goldfields routes
- Owen has been in the area 10+ years and is well-known locally

### Period 2 (c.1899-1901): Nellie Conceived
- Owen is 54-56, running the Family Hotel at Two Mile, Cooktown
- His last child with Mary Connors was 1891 (8-10 years earlier)
- An 1922 Cooktown Occurrence Book records Aboriginal people at the Two Mile - the exact location of Owen's hotel
- Rosie is 30-32, back in the Cooktown area (1896 hospital admission confirms she was there)
- Owen has opportunity through proximity: Aboriginal camp literally next to his hotel

### The Two Mile Connection
The **Two Mile** was:
- Where Owen ran the Family Hotel (1900-1913+)
- Where Aboriginal people camped (1922 Occurrence Book confirmed)
- On the road to Laura/Palmer River (Owen's original bullock route)
- Near the McIvor River (Rosie's birthplace)

This geographic overlap is NOT coincidental. Owen lived and worked at the exact location where Aboriginal people gathered. He had decades of contact with the Aboriginal community.

## The "Agnes" Question

One GOKS claim notes: "If this is the same Owen Reynolds, he had a child born in 1905 to a woman named 'Agnes' (not Mary Connors)." 

The Pioneers & Settlers Register lists an **Agnes Maud Reynolds** (born c.1905). If Owen fathered a child with "Agnes" in 1905 (age 60), this shows he was still having children with women other than Mary Connors into his 60s. This pattern is consistent with fathering Nellie (c.1899-1901, age 54-56) and Edgar (c.1883, age 38).
