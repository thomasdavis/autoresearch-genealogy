## The 1905 Removal: Key Details

This is the most important record for Nellie. From the Chief Protector's Annual Report 1905:

> "Nellie, half-caste, four and a half years, an orphan, Rossville. The woman in charge of her was stated to have been 'in the habit of drinking too much community.' Sent on the Minister's order to Yarrabah."

**Critical details:**
- **"An orphan"**: Her mother (Rosie) is dead by 1905. This SUPPORTS the 1904 death.
- **"Half-caste"**: Nellie is classified H/C, meaning one Aboriginal parent and one non-Aboriginal parent. This confirms Owen Reynolds (European) as her father.
- **"Rossville"**: Near Cooktown, on the road to Laura. This is Owen Reynolds' territory.
- **"The woman in charge of her"**: NOT Rosie. Someone else was looking after Nellie. If Rosie was alive, she would be "the mother", not "the woman in charge."
- **Julia (age 9)** removed at the same time: Julia and Nellie may be sisters or were in the same household.

## The 1915 War Census: Nellie at Port Douglas

Nellie appears on page 138 of the Port Douglas War Census alongside:
- Rosie Honalle
- George (unnamed, immediately before Rosie)
- Fred Braikenridge (H/C)
- Mary
- Mick
- Yorkey

**If Nellie was born c.1900, she was ~15 in 1915** - listed as an "adult" (young women were often listed as adults from age 14-15 in Aboriginal communities).

**Connection to Rosie Honalle**: If our Rosie died in 1904, then this "Rosie Honalle" is a different woman. But Nellie is still listed in the same block, possibly because she grew up at Yarrabah with the Braikenridge/Brackenridge children and returned to Port Douglas as a teenager.

## Nellie and Edgar: Separated Siblings

| | Edgar | Nellie |
|--|-------|--------|

| Surname | Davis / Braikenridge | Watkins / Murgha / Owens |
| At Yarrabah | Baptised 1896, married 1909 | Arrived 1905, baptised 1906 |
| Overlap at Yarrabah | 1905-1909 (at minimum) | 1905 onwards |

**They were both at Yarrabah from 1905 to at least 1909.** Edgar married Caroline there in October 1909. Nellie arrived as a 4-year-old orphan in 1905.

But Edgar was "Edgar Davis" or "Edgar Braikenridge" and Nellie was "Nellie Watkins". Different surnames, different ages (Edgar 15-26 years older), and no recorded parent linking them. **They almost certainly did not know they were half-siblings.**

Edgar was writing articles for the Yarrabah newspaper in May 1908. Nellie was a 7-8 year old child at the same mission. He may have known her as a child at the mission, but not as his sister.
