# Was Julia Rosie's Daughter? Evidence Review

## Evidence FOR Julia Being Rosie's Daughter

### 1. Direct Family Testimony
- **Fred Leftwich** (Julia's SON, heard directly from Julia): "Nellie Watkins was Julia's sister"
- Julia herself said "the Nunn family looked after her after her mother died"
- This is first-generation oral testimony, not distant family legend

### 2. Marriage Record
- Julia's 1912 Yarrabah marriage: mother listed as **"Rosie Rosie"**
- Mission staff recorded the biological parents, not foster parents
- Father listed as **"Davis"** (not "Nunn")

### 3. Naming Pattern
- Julia named TWO daughters after Rosie: **Bessie Rosina** (1916) and **Rosina Rice**
- "Rosina" = the name on Edgar's death certificate for his mother
- Naming children after a dead mother is powerful evidence of a real parent-child bond

### 4. Co-removal from Rossville
- Julia (age 9) and Nellie (age 4.5) removed together from Rossville in 1905
- Both listed as "orphans" from the same location
- Both described as H/C (half-caste)

### 5. Geography
- Julia born at **Romeo** (near Rossville/Bloomfield) - same area as Nellie
- Rosie recorded at Mossman (1899, 1901), Cooktown (1896), and the Bloomfield/Rossville area is between them
- All within Kuku Yalanji country

### 6. Kuku Yalanji Identity
- Julia described as "Kuku-Yaianji" (Eastern Kuku Yalanji) - same as Rosie's identified clan

### 7. "Rosie Rosie" in Native Title
- "Rosie Rosie" appears in the Eastern Kuku Yalanji native title claimant framework as a named individual
- This suggests "Rosie Rosie" was a real person known to the Kuku Yalanji community, not an arbitrary name

## Evidence AGAINST or Uncertain

### 1. Different Father
- Julia's father: **"Davis"** (marriage record)
- Edgar's father: **Owen Reynolds** (DNA proven)
- Nellie's father: **Owen Reynolds** (DNA proven)
- If Julia's father is different, she's a HALF-sibling, not full sibling

### 2. "Sister" in Aboriginal Kinship
- "Sister" in Aboriginal kinship systems can mean classificatory sister (clan sister, cousin)
- Fred Leftwich may have used "sister" in a kinship sense, not biological
- BUT: he specifically named "Nellie Watkins" by her mission name, suggesting a specific personal relationship

### 3. No DNA Confirmation
- No Leftwich descendants have tested on Ancestry DNA
- We cannot verify the relationship through DNA
- The Cecil Copeland Leftwich family at Mossman is the best lead for DNA testing

### 4. Julia's Baptism Lists No Parents
- Julia's 1906 Yarrabah baptism: father blank, mother blank
- Consistent with being an orphan, but doesn't confirm who her mother was

### 5. Multiple "Rosies" at Yarrabah
- There were several women named Rosie at Yarrabah
- "Rosie Mosman" (Vera Yinigie's mother, 1908), "Rosie Shoreman" (Robert Eldon's mother, 1910), "Rosie Shoman" (adult baptism 1911), "Rosie Brackenridge" (Frederick's mother, 1899)
- These could be the same woman or different women

## DNA Math: What Would Julia's Descendants Look Like?

If Julia and Edgar are half-siblings (shared mother Rosie, different fathers):
- They share ~25% DNA (half-sibling through one parent)
- Julia's grandchild shares ~6.25% of Julia's DNA
- Lisa (Edgar's great-granddaughter) shares ~3.1% of Rosie's DNA
- **Expected shared DNA between Lisa and Julia's grandchild: ~20-60 cM**

The **Hall/Jones/Kyle cluster** (Carol Hall 170, Candice 116, Kailee 54, Corinne 41) is TOO HIGH for Julia's line through Rosie alone. These likely connect through **Molloy/Roberts or Noble/Hewison** instead.

Julia's descendants (if they've tested) would be at **20-50 cM** level - in the range of the unidentified matches like rly1950, Mike Whiteley, etc. But we can't distinguish them from Molloy/Noble matches without a confirmed Leftwich test.

## The Key Test

**One DNA test from a Leftwich descendant would resolve everything:**

1. If they share ~20-60 cM with Lisa AND share DNA with Hezron Murgha (Nellie's line) → Julia connects through Rosie (half-sibling confirmed)
2. If they share ~20-60 cM with Lisa but DON'T share with Hezron Murgha → Julia connects through Rosie but may have a different relationship to Nellie
3. If they share <10 cM or no DNA with Lisa → Julia is NOT Rosie's daughter (unrelated)

## The Rosie Shoman Problem

The Yarrabah baptism register records an **adult Rosie Shoman H/C** baptised November 1911, "from Mareeba."

If this is our Rosie:
- She was ALIVE in 1911 (7 years after the supposed 1904 death)
- "Shoman" is a variant of "Shoreman" (Rosie Shoreman, Robert Eldon's mother, 1910)
- "From Mareeba" matches Rosie's 1942 indigence application birthplace
- She's H/C (half-caste) - matches Rosie's classification

If this is our Rosie AND she's Julia's mother, then:
- Rosie did NOT die in 1904
- Rosie was at Yarrabah in 1910-1911 while Julia and Nellie were also there
- Rosie would have been with her daughters, not dead

BUT: Julia said "her mother died" and the Nunn family took her in. If Rosie was alive at Yarrabah in 1911, why did Julia think her mother was dead? 

Possible answers:
- "Rosie Shoman" is a DIFFERENT Rosie
- Julia was separated from Rosie at a young age and was told her mother had died
- "Mother died" in Julia's oral history might mean "my mother was taken away from me" (which was a form of social death under the Protection system)

## Assessment

| Question | Evidence | Probability |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Was Julia Rosie's biological daughter? | Fred Leftwich testimony, marriage record, naming pattern, co-removal, geography, clan | **75% likely** |
| Were Julia and Nellie half-siblings (same mother, different father)? | Marriage record says different father "Davis" vs Owen Reynolds for Nellie | **60% likely** (if Julia is Rosie's daughter) |
| Were Julia and Nellie full siblings (same mother AND father)? | Would require "Davis" = Owen Reynolds under another name | **15% likely** |
| Was Julia unrelated to Rosie? | Would require "sister" to be classificatory only and "Rosie Rosie" to be a different person | **25% likely** |

**Bottom line**: The weight of evidence strongly suggests Julia was Rosie's daughter, making her Edgar and Nellie's half-sister. But without DNA confirmation, we cannot be certain.
