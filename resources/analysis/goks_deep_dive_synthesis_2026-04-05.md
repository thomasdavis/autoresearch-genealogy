## DATABASE CAVEAT

The 286 CIFHS Queensland HTML pages (removals, deaths, marriages, leprosy, exemptions, protector cards, war census) were **NOT actually ingested**. The batch process logged 281 files as "completed" but stored zero documents, chunks, or claims. The false log entries have been cleared. All findings below come from the 164 previously ingested documents (DNA analysis, oral histories, BDM searches, gazette extracts, Yarrabah baptisms, and manually created CIFHS summaries).

## 2. ROSIE: THE IDENTITY PROBLEM

### The Alias Chain (Strong)
Rosie Homalee = Rosie Homarlee = Rosie Honalle = Rosie Hippie = Rosie Hippi = Rosie Epi = Rosie Julian

### The Unresolved Merge
**Rosie Brackenridge** (mother of Edgar, on birth registrations) and **Rosie Hippie/Julian** (died 1964) have NOT been conclusively proven to be the same woman. The merge requires a record naming a shared child or explicitly identifying Rosie Hippie as mother of Edgar Davis or the Brackenridge boys.

### Rosie Hippie/Julian Identity (Strong, 803 claims)
- Born c.1869, Mareeba area (from 1942 indigence application age ~73)
- Died 21 August 1964, District Hospital Mossman, age 95
- Death cert: Father "Mick" (no surname), Mother "Nellie"
- 1942 indigence application: widow of Willie Api (SSI from Epi Island), brother Tommy Gray at Mona Mona, "None" for sons or daughters, living in hut on Mr Frank Coulthard's land at Saltwater/Miallo
- Pension drawn under "ROSIE HOMARLEE EPI"
- Married Paddy Julian at Mossman, 13 May 1949 (BDM 1949/C/1596)
- QSA Ref COL/34, Item ID ITM18062, Digital Image DR112479

### Rosie Brackenridge Identity (Moderate, from birth registrations)
- Mother on Edgar's delayed birth registration 1906/O/924: "Rosie of McIvor River, age 16"
- Frederick Brackenridge baptism 1899: only child with both parents explicitly named (George Brackenridge and Rosie Brackenridge)
- George Brackenridge's partner

### The "None" Problem
The 1942 indigence application says "None" for sons or daughters. If this is the same Rosie who had Edgar, Nellie, Julia, Arthur, Frederick, Joseph, and possibly more children, why would she say "None"? Possible explanations:
1. She was estranged from all her children (many were removed by the state)
2. She had no children she considered legally "hers" under the Protection Act
3. She's a different Rosie
4. The form was filled in by someone else who didn't know her family

### Rosie's Partners (from GOKS)

2. Owen Reynolds (Irish bullock driver, Cooktown) - biological father of Edgar AND Nellie per DNA
3. Willie Api/Epi (SSI from Epi Island) - husband per 1942 indigence app
4. Paddy Julian (Mossman) - husband per 1949 marriage cert

### Rosie's Parents

- Alternative: **Jaoquan** and **JULOM** listed as parents of Rosie Homarlee in native title records

## 7. CRITICAL CLUSTERING: PORT DOUGLAS WAR CENSUS PAGE 138

The 1915 War Census (QSA DR25243, page 138) lists the following people TOGETHER in the Port Douglas district:
- **George** (no surname)
- **Rosie Honalle** (Female, Adult)
- **Fred Braikenridge** (H/C)
- **Nellie**
- **Mary**
- **Mick**
- **Yorkey**

This is a family group. George (Brackenridge?), Rosie, Fred Braikenridge (their son), Nellie (Rosie's mother?), Mary (unknown), and Mick (Rosie's father?) all in the same administrative record. This is one of the strongest pieces of evidence that these people were a recognized family unit as late as 1915.
