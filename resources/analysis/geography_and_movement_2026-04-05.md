## Kuku Yalanji Country

Rosie's people (Eastern Kuku Yalanji, Kunjandji clan) had country spanning ~400km of coastline from Pretty Beach (near Port Douglas) north to Annan River (near Cooktown). This means:

- McIvor River (Rosie's birthplace) = Kuku Yalanji country
- Rossville/Bloomfield = Kuku Yalanji country  
- Port Douglas/Mossman = southern edge of Kuku Yalanji country
- Cooktown = northern edge / Guugu Yimithirr border

Rosie would have known tracks connecting ALL these places. Movement between them was normal for her people.

## Where Was Nellie Born?

The 1905 removal record says Nellie was at **Rossville** when removed.

**Rossville is 26km from Cooktown.** It's basically Cooktown's back country. Owen Reynolds was AT Cooktown running the Family Hotel at Two Mile.

If Nellie was born c.1900-1901 at or near Rossville:
- Owen was 55-56, at the Family Hotel, Two Mile, Cooktown (26km away)
- Rosie was 31-32, and her location is uncertain - she was at Mossman in 1899 (Frederick baptism) and 1901 (Protector report) but could have traveled back to Cooktown
- Rossville is on the Bloomfield River, within Kuku Yalanji country

**The proximity of Rossville to Cooktown is striking.** Nellie was born/living essentially next door to Owen Reynolds.

## Movement Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Rosie Moved Between Cooktown and Mossman

```
c.1883: Rosie at McIvor River/Cooktown → Edgar born (Owen's territory)
c.1886-1899: Rosie moves south to Mossman/Port Douglas → with George Brackenridge
1896: Rosie briefly back at Cooktown (hospital)
1899: Rosie at Mossman (Frederick baptism)
c.1899-1901: Rosie back at Cooktown/Rossville → Nellie born (Owen's territory again)
1901: Rosie at Mossman again ("married to kanaka at Mossman")
1904: Rosie sick → steamer to Cairns Hospital → dies → buried Pioneer Cemetery
1905: Nellie still at Rossville, 4.5 years old, orphaned
```

This has Rosie ping-ponging between Cooktown and Mossman - 100km each way. Possible within Kuku Yalanji country but a lot of movement.

### Scenario 2: Rosie Stayed Mostly at Mossman/Port Douglas, Nellie Placed at Rossville

```
c.1883: Rosie at McIvor River → Edgar born
c.1886+: Rosie settles at Mossman/Port Douglas
c.1900: Nellie born at Mossman/Port Douglas
c.1901-1903: Nellie placed with someone at Rossville (maybe a relative, maybe a Protector arrangement)
c.1904: Rosie dies at Cairns (steamer from Port Douglas, few hours)
1905: Nellie at Rossville, listed as "orphan"
```

This has less movement for Rosie but requires explaining why Nellie was at Rossville, not Mossman.

### Scenario 3: Rosie Didn't Die in 1904

```
c.1883: Rosie at McIvor River → Edgar born
c.1886+: Moves between Cooktown and Mossman
c.1900: Nellie born (Rossville or Mossman area)
1904: A DIFFERENT Rosie buried at Pioneer Cemetery
1905: Nellie at Rossville - "orphan" is Protector language for "half-caste child we want to remove", not necessarily meaning mother is dead. The drinking woman could be the caretaker while Rosie works elsewhere.
1905: Rosie at Saltwater Creek, Port Douglas with "a son" - ALIVE
1907-1910: Rosie at Cooktown (hospital admissions)
1915: Rosie at Port Douglas (War Census)
```

## The Cairns Hospital Question

If Rosie died at **Cairns Base Hospital** in March 1904:
- From Mossman/Port Douglas: 67-75km. Regular steamer, few hours. Easy to transfer a sick person.
- From Cooktown: 330km. 1-2 day steamer. Possible but longer.
- From Rossville: No direct route. Would need to go to Cooktown first then steamer, or overland to Port Douglas then steamer. Difficult.

**Most likely**: If Rosie died at Cairns hospital, she was living at Mossman/Port Douglas at the time (short steamer trip to Cairns), not at Rossville.

But Nellie was at Rossville. This creates a gap:
- Rosie at Mossman (67km from Cairns, dying)
- Nellie at Rossville (26km from Cooktown, 250km from Cairns)

Who was looking after Nellie at Rossville while Rosie was at Mossman?

## Open Questions

1. **Why was Nellie at Rossville and not with Rosie at Mossman?** Distance: ~100km apart.
2. **Was Rosie ever at Rossville herself?** No record places her there specifically.
3. **Did Owen know about Nellie?** Rossville is 26km from his hotel. If Nellie was born there c.1900, Owen was right there.
4. **Could Owen have placed Nellie at Rossville?** He was a powerful local figure. He couldn't openly acknowledge a half-caste daughter but could arrange for her care nearby.
5. **Was "the woman in charge" at Rossville a relative of Rosie's?** The caretaker could be from the same Kuku Yalanji network.
6. **Julia (age 9, born c.1896) was also at Rossville.** Were Julia and Nellie both Rosie's children living with a caretaker while Rosie was at Mossman?
